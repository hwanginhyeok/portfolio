D1 · Stress-Strength Interference + Weibull / B10

1-A. Stress-Strength Interference

한 줄 정의: 부품 강도(Strength)와 부하(Stress)가 모두 분포를 가지며, 두 분포가 겹치는 영역이 고장 확률 $P_f$다. 
R = P(S > L) = ∫ f_S(s)·G_L(s) ds
Pf = 1 − R   (overlap 면적)
SF = μ_S / μ_L  ← 평균만 봄 (한계)
Stress-Strength Interference: 겹치는 노란 영역 = 고장 확률 Pf. 직접 제작 SVG.

1-B. Weibull 분포

f(t) = (β/η)·(t/η)^(β-1)·exp[-(t/η)^β]
R(t) = exp[-(t/η)^β]
MTTF = η·Γ(1 + 1/β)
B10  = η·(-ln 0.9)^(1/β) ≈ η·(0.1054)^(1/β)
β고장 모드Bathtub대응 전략
<1초기 불량 (h↓)Infant mortalityBurn-in
=1우발 고장 (h=const)Useful lifeRedundancy
>1마모 고장 (h↑)Wear-outPM / redesign
Bathtub Curve: β와 구간의 1:1 매핑. 직접 제작 SVG.
Weibull PDF — β=0.5/1/1.5/5 형상 비교. 출처: Wikipedia Commons (public domain).
황인혁 경험 연결
RBDO Lab 석사 2년 → Stress-Strength Interference는 RBDO의 기반.
IGBT PCT bond-wire lift-off → β>1 (wear-out) 확인 (P-01 IEEE TIM 2024).
Q: "How would you set a reliability target for a new actuator?"
"I'd start by treating both load and strength as distributions — that's stress-strength interference. The overlap area is the failure probability. For lifetime, I'd fit Weibull to ALT data and report B10 instead of mean — for safety-critical parts, the early failure tail matters more than the average. The shape parameter β tells me whether I'm dealing with infant mortality, random failures, or wear-out, which determines whether I need burn-in, redundancy, or design margin."

Follow-up (클릭 펼치기)

What's the difference between Pf and 1/MTTF?
Pf는 누적 확률 (시간 종속). 1/MTTF는 평균 고장률. β=1일 때만 동치.
Why B10 not B50?
산업 표준 (베어링/모터). Safety-critical은 B1 또는 B0.1 사용.
How do you estimate β with censored data?
MLE (Maximum Likelihood Estimation). Median Rank Regression은 small sample.

2-B. Coffin-Manson + 가속 인자

Nf = A · (ΔT)^(-n)   [온도 기반 단순화형]
AF = (ΔT_test / ΔT_field)^n

예: ΔT_field=20°C, ΔT_test=100°C, n=2
   AF = (100/20)^2 = 25
   → 시험 1,000 cycles = 필드 25,000 cycles
Coffin-Manson log-log plot: 직선 기울기 = -n. 직접 제작 SVG.
PCT ≠ Coffin-Manson 혼용 금지
PCT = 시험 프로토콜 (어떻게 테스트하는가)
Coffin-Manson = 수명 외삽 모델 (테스트 데이터로 수명 계산)

PCT 수행 → 데이터 수집 → Coffin-Manson으로 외삽 = 올바른 순서

Q: "How would you select a JESD47 test for a new power module?"
"I'd start with the failure mechanism. For bond-wire interconnects, thermal fatigue is dominant — so Power Cycling per JESD47 is the primary test. PCT controls junction temperature directly, unlike TC which controls ambient. To translate PCT data to field life, you apply Coffin-Manson: N_f = A·ΔT^(-n). The acceleration factor is (ΔT_test/ΔT_field)^n — typically n=2 for solder. Key: PCT is the test protocol, Coffin-Manson is the life model. I ran PCT on IGBT modules at the lab — bond-wire lift-off showed β > 1, classic wear-out, which validates Coffin-Manson applicability."
TC vs PC 차이?
TC = 챔버 공기 온도 제어. PC = 전류로 접합 직접 가열. TC는 솔더/패키지, PC는 본드와이어/die-attach 타겟.
Activation energy Ea는 어떻게 결정?
복수 온도에서 HTOL → ln(MTTF) vs 1/T 직선 기울기 = Ea/k. 문헌값 참조도 일반적.

Q: "How would you specify environmental tests for an outdoor agricultural robot?"
"Outdoor, dusty fields, water spray, temperature extremes, rough terrain — maps to: IP66/67 (IEC 60529), IEC 60068-2 Ab/Bb for temperature, thermal shock via IEC 60068-2 N, random vibration via IEC 60068-2 Fh. On GT-SS500, DFMEA told me where to focus: pump O-ring freeze at -40°C and GND noise from vibration — always design the test matrix around the failure mechanisms."
IP68 vs IP67 핵심 차이?
IP67 = 1m/30min (IEC 기본). IP68 = 제조사가 더 깊은/긴 조건 지정 (Apple: 6m/30min). IP68이 IP67을 포함.
Thermal Shock vs Temperature Cycling 차이?
Thermal Shock (IEC 2-14 N): 급격한 전환 (수 초~수 분) → 세라믹·솔더 크랙. TC (IEC 2-30 Db): 느린 전환 → 크리프 피로. 타겟 메커니즘 다름.

5-B. Cpk / Ppk 산식

Cp  = (USL - LSL) / 6σ_within
Cpk = min[ (USL - μ)/3σ_within,  (μ - LSL)/3σ_within ]

Pp  = (USL - LSL) / 6σ_overall
Ppk = min[ (USL - μ)/3σ_overall,  (μ - LSL)/3σ_overall ]

Cpk > Ppk → 공정 드리프트 존재

Q: "How do you handle small-sample reliability testing?"
"When sample sizes are small — fewer than twenty units — frequentist MLE gives wide confidence intervals on B10. Bayesian reliability addresses this by incorporating a prior: historical data from a predecessor component, or Jeffreys non-informative prior. The posterior is narrower, and you extract a credible interval on B10 directly. I haven't applied this in production yet, but it's a natural extension of RBDO — where we used prior strength distributions before test data arrived. On the production side, Cpk quantifies whether the process is centered and capable. Cpk 1.33 is the baseline: four sigma clearance. Ppk adds long-term drift. If Cpk exceeds Ppk, your process is drifting."
Cpk vs Ppk — when does the gap matter?
Cpk > Ppk → 드리프트. 설비 교체·배치 간 원자재 변동 의심. Cpk ≈ Ppk → 공정 안정.

STAR #5 — Damage Summation PV Polymer (Solar Energy 2024)

Miner's RuleD = Σ(n_i/N_i) D=1.0 고장Solar Energy 2024 (4저자)
S건국대 학부 연구원 2023. PV 폴리머 수명 예측. 단일 스트레스 모델 = 과보수적.
TMiner's Rule로 변동환경 스트레스 이력 통합, 정확도 개선.
A필드 온도·습도 시계열 → 스트레스 구간별 N_i 계산 → D = Σ(n_i/N_i) 적산. D=1.0 = 수명 종료. 단일 스트레스 모델 및 필드 관찰 데이터와 비교.
RDamage Summation이 필드와 훨씬 정합. Solar Energy 2024 출판.
외울 1단락:
"I discretized the field stress history into bins — each a temperature-humidity combination with a measured duration. For each bin I computed N_i from the single-stress model, then accumulated the damage fraction n_i/N_i. When the sum reached 1.0, predicted end-of-life. The damage summation model aligned much more closely with field observations than worst-case constant-stress predictions. Published in Solar Energy, Elsevier, 2024. Directly applicable to smartphone camera modules exposed to daily temperature and humidity cycles."
