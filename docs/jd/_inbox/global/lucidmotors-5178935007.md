Position Overview: The Maintenance Manager is responsible for leading the Maintenance team to ensure equipment reliability, uptime, and production continuity across the shop. This role supports new product introductions, production ramp-up, and steady-state operations by implementing robust preventive and predictive maintenance strategies, managing equipment readiness, and driving continuous improvement in maintenance performance. The position plays a critical role in achieving production KPIs, minimizing downtime, and ensuring safe and efficient operation of all equipment and systems. &nbsp; Key Responsibilities: o Maintenance Strategy and Execution: • Develop and implement preventive and predictive maintenance programs for GA equipment and production systems. • Ensure all equipment is maintained to meet safety, quality, and operational standards. • Oversee maintenance execution to maximize uptime and minimize unplanned downtime. • Establish maintenance schedules aligned with production requirements and launch timelines. o Equipment Readiness and Launch Support: • Support new program launches by ensuring equipment installation, commissioning, and readiness. • Partner with Manufacturing Engineering during equipment handover and validation phases. • Ensure maintenance readiness for new product introduction and production ramp-up. • Participate in equipment acceptance tests (SAT/FAT) and commissioning activities. o Team Leadership and Resource Management: • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Maintenance Engineers, Technicians, and Specialists. • Define priorities and allocate resources across shifts and production schedules. • Build technical capability within the team to support advanced manufacturing systems. • Drive accountability and performance management within the maintenance organization. o Equipment Reliability and Continuous Improvement • Monitor equipment performance and drive improvements in MTBF, MTTR, and overall reliability. • Lead root cause analysis for equipment failures using structured problem-solving tools. • Implement corrective and preventive actions to eliminate recurring breakdowns. • Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance equipment efficiency and lifecycle. o Performance and KPI Management • Track and report maintenance KPIs including uptime, downtime, response time, and cost efficiency. • Identify gaps in maintenance performance and implement improvement plans. • Support production teams to achieve overall plant throughput and efficiency targets. o Safety and Compliance: • Ensure all maintenance activities are performed in compliance with safety regulations and standards. • Promote a strong safety culture within the maintenance team and across the GA shop. • Ensure compliance with environmental, health, and safety (EHS) policies and procedures. o Cross-Functional Collaboration: • Collaborate with Production, Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, and Facilities teams. • Support process improvements by ensuring equipment capability aligns with manufacturing needs. • Provide technical input during design-for-manufacturing (DFM) and process development reviews. Skills: o Technical Skills: • Strong knowledge of automotive assembly equipment and systems • Expertise in preventive and predictive maintenance strategies • Understanding of PLC systems, automation, robotics, and control systems • Experience with CMMS systems and maintenance planning tools • Knowledge of equipment reliability metrics (MTBF, MTTR) and analysis techniques o Personal Skills: • Strong leadership and team development capability • Analytical and structured problem-solving skills • Effective communication and cross-functional collaboration • Ability to manage high-pressure situations during production and launch phases • Results-oriented with strong ownership and accountability Specific Requirements • Flexibility to support off-shift and weekend activities during launch and ramp-up phases • Experience supporting new product launches and commissioning activities preferred Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or related field&nbsp; • Minimum 8–10 years of experience in maintenance engineering or manufacturing operations within automotive or high-volume production environments • Proven experience in equipment maintenance, reliability improvement, and production support • Hands-on experience with maintenance systems, automation equipment, and troubleshooting • Strong leadership experience managing technical teams in a manufacturing environment&nbsp; Compensation &amp; Benefits: Lucid offers a comprehensive and competitive&nbsp; benefits package &nbsp;including medical, dental, and vision insurance; life and disability coverage; paid time off; paid holidays; and a&nbsp;401(k) retirement&nbsp;plan.
