## JD 본문

Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential. Agility Robotics views safety not as a regulatory constraint, but as a fundamental commercial gateway that is absolutely required to move humanoid robots out of isolated work cells and into shared human environments. You can't build your robot and then make it safe - functional safety must be baked into the hardware, electrical systems, and software from day one. The Role We are seeking a Senior Safety Software Engineer to play a critical role in implementing a functionally safe perception system.&nbsp; In this role, you will bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and functional safety principles.&nbsp; You will work closely with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and validate safety-critical software architecture. If you are passionate about building robust systems that push the boundaries of modern robotics and automation while maintaining uncompromising safety, we want you on our team. Key Responsibilities Design &amp; Develop: Design and implement robust, highly reliable software for safety-critical systems using both traditional functional safety techniques and emerging deep learning safety techniques. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with our machine learning, hardware, and core software teams to ensure that data pipelines, models, and middleware operate within defined safety envelopes. System Architecture: Develop and optimize software running on both Linux and real-time operating systems. Testing &amp; Validation: Create rigorous testing frameworks and validation strategies to prove system reliability and safety under edge-case scenarios. Mentorship: Guide and mentor junior engineers on safety-critical coding standards and best practices. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or a related field. 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a focus on systems level or embedded systems programming (C++, C, or Rust).
