You will quickly learn our technology and the world of vehicle regulations as you first support, and then conduct under supervision, in-house tests to demonstrate compliance before leading certain technical fields. You must have strong attention to detail and a proactive mindset as well as being able to deliver projects on time in order to support production timelines and market introductions. &nbsp; &nbsp; What You Own &nbsp; &nbsp; Interpret, document, and cascade regulatory requirements to cross-functional engineering teams &nbsp; Create and maintain regulatory application documents for different markets. &nbsp; Conduct and support in-house and third-party regulatory testing for vehicle systems and functions. &nbsp; Involve in technical discussions with regulatory authorities, technical services, and internal engineering teams for chassis, active safety and ADAS systems. &nbsp; Support Functional Safety (ISO 26262) and SOTIF (ISO 21448) compliance activities as they relate to regulatory approval and market access requirements. &nbsp; Track running changes and ensure timely reporting. &nbsp; Develop working knowledge of existing and future automotive regulations. &nbsp; Participate in homologation program management, helping to effectively plan for upcoming changes. &nbsp; Assist manufacturing team with tasks such as labelling, COP, annual Homologation updates, etc. &nbsp; &nbsp; What You Bring &nbsp; Minimum Qualification s &nbsp; BS or MS degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Aerospace Engineering or related field. &nbsp; General understanding of automotive systems, vehicle development processes, and industry practices. &nbsp; Basic understanding of electric vehicle (EV) architecture. &nbsp; Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional engineering and program teams. &nbsp; Excellent communication skills (able to clearly communicate with internal and external stakeholders). &nbsp; Ability to travel domestically as needed, with potential for some international travel. &nbsp; Ability to work effectively under time-sensitive conditions and meet project milestones &nbsp; Valid US Driver’s license. &nbsp; &nbsp; Preferred Qualifications &nbsp; Experience in automotive regulatory compliance and certification activities, including FMVSS, UNECE/ECE, CMVSS, and other global vehicle regulations. &nbsp; Experience supporting vehicle-level testing or validation. &nbsp; Familiarity with data acquisition and test instrumentation systems such as LINK V-MAX, Dewesoft, IPETRONIK, and similar platforms. &nbsp; Familiarity with CAN communication, diagnostics, and calibration tools, including Vector CANoe/CANalyzer, Vector VN interfaces, and related equipment. &nbsp; Familiarity with Functional Safety (ISO 26262) and Safety of the Intended Functionality (ISO 21448/SOTIF) concepts and their application to vehicle systems. &nbsp; Experience working with regulatory authorities, technical services, and certification agencies such as NHTSA, EPA, CARB, Transport Canada, UNECE approval authorities, and similar organizations. &nbsp;
