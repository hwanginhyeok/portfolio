SPG Refurbishment BD Program Management – Korea Key Responsibilities Prepare project plans and drive the project from conception and planning to implementation. Participate in the development of strategies, processes, and resources. Manage project schedule and task details by utilizing project management tools such as reports, tracking charts, checklists, and project scheduling software. Coordinate allocated resources from within the Division to achieve on-time and within-budget performance objectives. Initiate and manage purchase requisitions and purchase orders. Drive cost and schedule performance of equipment for internal and external customers and vendors worldwide. Participate on multifunctional design teams (internal, external, and/or matrixed) to complete projects; provide technical input to team members to achieve project goals. Interface and coordinate within the Division to define project objectives, provide status updates, and prepare for release and deployment. Provide engineering and business process expertise to team and other members on various standard programs/issues. Identify and resolve potential complications within the project and develop solutions for resolution.
