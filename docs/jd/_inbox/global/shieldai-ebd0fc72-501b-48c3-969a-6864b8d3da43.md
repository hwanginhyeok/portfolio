Follow Shield AI on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and YouTube. Job Description: Shield AI is seeking a Senior C++ Software Engineer, Edge Systems to define and build the software foundations that power autonomous systems operating in demanding real-world environments. This role sits within the Edge Systems team, where software meets hardware. You will solve complex engineering problems spanning Linux edge computing, imaging, sensor integration, mission systems, and autonomy, building software that runs on real hardware in operational environments rather than laboratory prototypes. As a Senior Staff Engineer, you will provide technical leadership across the Edge Systems domain. You will shape architecture and long-term technical direction across multiple teams and products, lead the resolution of complex cross-cutting problems, and establish engineering approaches that improve the effectiveness of the broader organisation. This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You will continue to design, write, review, test, and debug production software, while focusing your individual contribution on the organisation’s highest-risk and highest-leverage technical problems. You will develop modern C++ software for Linux-based edge compute platforms, including NVIDIA Jetson-class and ARM64 systems. Your work will span sensor integration, imaging pipelines, calibration, diagnostics, deployment, developer tooling, and mission-critical applications that demand high performance, reliability, and maintainability. This is an application and systems software role focused on Linux-based edge computing, rather than bare-metal firmware or microcontroller development. No two weeks look the same. One day you might be optimising image-processing pipelines on embedded GPUs. The next you could be defining architecture for future autonomous platforms, leading the integration of new sensors, or collaborating with AI researchers to bring new capabilities into production. You will work alongside highly capable systems, hardware, autonomy, AI, product, and test engineers who enjoy solving difficult problems together.
