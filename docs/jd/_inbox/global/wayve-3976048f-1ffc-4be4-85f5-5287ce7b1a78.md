## JD 본문

Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Technical Program Management Teams Our Technical Program Management team helps Wayve deliver complex, cross-functional initiatives with clarity and purpose. As our Lead Technical Program Manager for Quality & Compliance, you’ll build and lead a small, high-impact TPM team that rolls out quality and compliance initiatives across the company — spanning quality (including ASPICE), security, data privacy and operational safety. You’ll help us meet heavyweight requirements through pragmatic, low-overhead processes that raise the bar without slowing teams down. 🧠 YOUR DAY-TO-DAY - Lead, coach and grow a small TPM team, hiring to fill gaps and raising the bar on program delivery. - Drive flagship quality and compliance initiatives yourself while supporting your team to deliver across Wayve. - Partner with engineering, product, safety, security, privacy / legal and operations leaders, influencing without authority at every level. - Turn complex requirements into simple, practical processes; negotiate must-haves versus nice-to-haves and find creative ways to meet long-standing obligations. - Manage dependencies, risks and trade-offs, adapting your approach in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment. - Use metrics and evidence to steer delivery, communicate progress and represent quality and compliance in company-level reviews. 🧩 WHAT YOU’LL BE WORKING ON: - Rolling out company-wide quality and compliance initiatives — including ASPICE, security, data privacy and operational safety — from framework to embedded practice. - Building a scalable TPM function that combines strong people leadership with hands-on delivery. - Establishing governance, KPIs, dashboards, audit readiness, escalations and operational reviews that create clarity without unnecessary bureaucracy. - Designing lightweight processes that work across teams and make compliance part of how Wayve operates. - Connecting quality and compliance investment to measurable outcomes: audit readiness, risk reduction and organisational efficiency. 🙌 YOU SHOULD APPLY IF: - You bring 8+ years of hands-on experience delivering quality, compliance or process programs as a technical program manager, alongside people and process leadership. You’ve built or scaled a program function and have a strong bias for action. - You’ve directly managed, coached and grown high-performing teams, including responsibility for performance management. - You’re pragmatic and creative: you can distinguish must-haves from nice-to-haves and find simpler ways to meet complex requirements. - You’re an exceptional communicator and influencer who brings people along across functions and levels, even without formal authority. - You’re skilled at designing simple, low-overhead processes and thinking through how changes affect the wider organisation. - You stay neutral and adapt under pressure, bringing structure to ambiguity without slowing delivery. - You’re product-minded and data-informed, prioritising by impact and linking programs to business outcomes rather than activity alone. - You’re open to feedback and continually looking for ways to improve. Experience with automotive, safety-critical, security or privacy standards — such as ASPICE, ISO 26262, SOTIF (ISO 21448), ISO 27001 or GDPR — would be helpful, but isn’t essential. A background in autonomous vehicles, robotics or another regulated domain, and an engineering or computer science degree or experience working as an engineer, would also be valuable. 🌱 Not ticking every box? That’s totally okay! If you’re passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every requirement. MORE ABOUT WAYVE: 🚀 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end to end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale. Instead of relying on hand coded rules or pre mapped environments, our AI Driver learns to drive by understanding the world around it. The result is technology that navigates complex urban environments with intelligence, precision and natural flow, unlocking meaningful advances in both safety and efficiency. We believe autonomy represents a once in a generation transformation in how people and goods move, comparable to the shift from horses to cars, and from human driven vehicles to intelligent machines. Our ambition is to make autonomy universal. Wayve’s mapless and hardware agnostic AI platform integrates with global OEM partners, enabling continuous software evolution and unlocking advanced levels of automation from L2 plus through to L4 as our core AI model scales. In a race increasingly defined by intelligence and real world learning, Wayve is taking a distinct approach, building a generalisable driving intelligence that can power any vehicle, anywhere. By combining embodied AI with scalable deployment, we are creating technology that can be shaped to each OEM brand and driver experience, accelerating the transition to a safer, more intelligent future of mobility.
