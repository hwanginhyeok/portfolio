## JD 본문

Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA. We are seeking a New Product Introduction (NPI) Engineer to oversee and drive the successful execution of prototype and pilot production builds. This role requires a strong leader who can coordinate cross-functional teams, manage materials and resources, maintain schedules, and ensure a smooth production process from start to finish. The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced environment and has a hands-on approach to problem-solving. Responsibilities: Manage the MBOM (Manufacturing Bill of Materials): Ensure accuracy, completeness, and availability of all components needed for the build. Change Management: Perform or coordinate change impact analysis, track implementation of ECNs and design changes. Oversee Prototype Builds: Track and coordinate the arrival of all required materials, consumables, and staffing resources necessary for the build. Act as primary DRI for coordinating all build activities.&nbsp; Develop &amp; Maintain Build Schedule: Create and enforce timelines, ensuring alignment with production goals and deadlines. Record &amp; Track Issues: Maintain detailed documentation of all issues encountered during the build, from component failures to process inefficiencies. Drive Issue Resolution: Ensure all Directly Responsible Individuals (DRIs) address and resolve issues in a timely manner, pushing for root cause analysis and corrective actions. Requirements:&nbsp; Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, or a related field (or equivalent experience). 5+ years of experience in NPI, manufacturing, or project management. Strong understanding of manufacturing processes, BOM management, and supply chain logistics. Understanding of PLM/ERP/MRP systems, scheduling tools, and data analysis. Strong communication and leadership skills with the ability to drive accountability. &nbsp; The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $135,000 - $200,000 annually.
