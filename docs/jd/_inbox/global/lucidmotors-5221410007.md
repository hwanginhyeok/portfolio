About the Team Lucid’s Field Quality Laboratory plays a critical role in understanding customer-facing quality concerns and turning evidence into durable engineering solutions. The team investigates returned components, vehicle data, laboratory findings, and field performance trends to determine why failures occur and how to prevent recurrence. Its work connects Field Quality, Warranty Engineering, Product Engineering, Reliability, Manufacturing Engineering, Supplier Quality, and Service Operations to accelerate issue resolution across Lucid’s vehicle programs. What makes this team unique is its hands-on, evidence-based approach to solving complex technical problems that directly influence customer experience, product quality, and future design decisions. About the Role As a Senior Field Quality Laboratory Engineer, Root Cause Analysis (RCA) , you will lead complex technical investigations involving customer-returned parts, failed components, vehicle systems, and field quality concerns. This role reports to a Field Quality Laboratory leadership role and exists to improve Lucid’s ability to identify validated failure mechanisms, reduce investigation cycle time, and prevent recurring quality issues. In your first 12 months, you will own high-impact investigations, strengthen repeatable failure-analysis methods, and help improve the quality of technical evidence used to support corrective action decisions. Success will be measured by clear root cause conclusions, effective corrective action validation, faster investigation turnaround, and stronger cross-functional alignment on issue resolution. A strong candidate will gain direct ownership of failure analysis for customer-impacting vehicle concerns, with access to returned components, diagnostic data, laboratory testing, and engineering decision-making across hardware, software, service, supplier, and manufacturing interfaces. What You'll Own Root Cause Ownership: Lead technical investigations into customer-reported quality concerns and determine validated failure mechanisms using returned components, vehicle data, service findings, and laboratory test results. Failure Analysis: Perform component inspections, teardown analysis, dimensional measurements, material evaluations, and functional assessments to identify failure origins and contributing factors. Validation Testing: Develop and execute structured test plans that reproduce field failures, validate hypotheses, and confirm the effectiveness of corrective actions. Technical Evidence: Build clear, evidence-based technical cases that combine laboratory findings, engineering data, vehicle diagnostics, manufacturing records, supplier information, and field performance data. Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner with Field Quality, Warranty Engineering, Product Engineering, Reliability, Manufacturing Engineering, Supplier Quality, and Service Operations to move investigations from issue identification through validated resolution. Process Standards: Create repeatable failure-analysis procedures, test methods, technical documentation, and lessons learned that improve investigation quality and consistency across the laboratory. Escalation Support: Provide technical expertise, investigation findings, and recommended actions for high-priority quality reviews, customer concerns, and emerging issue assessments.
