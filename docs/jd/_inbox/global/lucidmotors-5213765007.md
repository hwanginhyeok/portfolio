The team combines target setting, detailed engineering analysis, models, measurements, and cross-functional issue leadership to prevent performance creep. It partners with every major vehicle system and program team from architecture through launch. Its work helps Lucid deliver class-leading efficiency without compromising performance, comfort, or capability. &nbsp; About the Role &nbsp; As a Job Level 7 Mass Engineer, you will independently own defined mass tracking, correlation, and optimization workstreams for current and future vehicle programs. Reporting to a Manager of Vehicle Efficiency Engineering, you will connect engineering-system data and physical measurements to clear forecasts, risks, and actions. In your first 12 months, success means accurate and traceable mass data, dependable prototype and manufacturing correlation, and quantified reduction opportunities adopted by engineering owners. Lucid offers a vehicle-wide role where rigorous analysis directly protects range, performance, compliance, and customer value. &nbsp; What You Own &nbsp; Mass Data: Maintain vehicle, system, and option-level mass properties by reconciling CAD, PLM, BOM, MES, CAE, configuration, and measurement data. Integrate data from engineering systems and benchmarking databases to generate actionable insights and technical recommendations. &nbsp; Targets and Forecasts: Own defined target and forecasting workstreams, identify variance early, and maintain clear assumptions and confidence levels. Lead mass strategy activities including target setting, forecasting, benchmarking, and optimization across vehicle programs while balancing mass, cost, performance, manufacturing constraints, and customer requirements. &nbsp; Physical Correlation: Support prototype and manufacturing weigh events, investigate calculated-to-measured discrepancies, and drive traceable corrective actions. Lead component- and vehicle-level correlation activities to validate actual vehicle weights, improve mass accuracy, and ensure alignment between analytical and measured results. &nbsp; Test Support: Define and document vehicle weight conditions, payload, ballast, and mass assumptions for assigned validation, certification, durability, efficiency, or performance tests. Partner with Vehicle Integration, Validation, and Test teams to support regulatory and product development activities. &nbsp; Optimization: Analyze designs and benchmarks and develop practical mass-reduction opportunities that balance cost, performance, manufacturability, quality, and timing. Drive integrated product solutions that optimize vehicle efficiency while meeting program objectives and customer expectations. &nbsp; Decision Tools: Build clear dashboards, analyses, automated workflows, and AI-enabled solutions that improve engineering efficiency, mass visibility, traceability, and decision-making. Communicate risks, tradeoffs, and recommendations to engineering, program, and leadership teams. &nbsp; Technical Leadership: Influence cross-functional teams and stakeholders through data-driven communication, technical expertise, and a continuous improvement mindset while delivering results independently in a fast-paced development environment. &nbsp;
