As a&nbsp;Senior Quality Engineer, Electronics &amp; Vision Systems, you will lead all quality engineering activities for Electronics and Vision Systems at AMP2 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA). You will operate in a dynamic, fast‑paced manufacturing environment, working alongside a highly motivated, cross‑functional team on complex and high‑impact vehicle programs. In this role, you will partner across the entire product lifecycle to proactively protect Lucid’s production lines from quality risks. You will collaborate closely with Supplier Quality, Product Engineering, Manufacturing, Launch, and Field Quality teams to ensure robust quality execution from development through launch and sustained production. &nbsp; You Will Ensure compliance of quality deliverables at each project milestone during the launch phase in alignment with corporate standards Drive quality test execution and validation follow‑up in partnership with Product Engineering Review, validate, and support APQP/PPAP activities with Supplier Quality and Product Engineering Ensure part maturity and readiness to secure stable and efficient production lines Monitor responsiveness with Incoming Quality and suppliers on non‑conforming or defective parts Define Incoming Quality Inspection requirements and support training of internal teams and third‑party resources Collect, track, and resolve Incoming Quality Inspection findings during launch to protect production continuity Proactively identify supplier risks and lead mitigation actions in collaboration with Supplier Quality Development Drive problem‑solving activities with the Launch team; validate implementation and effectiveness through Gemba and ensure long‑term sustainability Lead containment and root‑cause resolution for internal quality issues identified through Pre‑Delivery Inspection (PDI) and Lucid Customer Product Audit findings Improve customer experience by leading 0MiS, 3MiS, 6MiS, and similar quality assessments, including containment and corrective actions, in coordination with Field Quality, Supplier Quality, Product Engineering, and Manufacturing Ensure readiness and approvals for New Parts Introduction (NPI), including trials, parts change management, and PPAP approvals Monitor and track Engineering Change Requests (ECRs) within PLM or equivalent systems &nbsp; You Bring Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering, Quality Engineering, Total Quality Management, or a related discipline 5+ years of relevant professional experience in quality or manufacturing engineering 5+ years of experience in a greenfield OEM environment (required) Minimum of 3 years of experience in a similar senior or system‑level quality engineering role (required) Strong verbal, written, and presentation skills Proven ability to influence, motivate, and gain alignment across cross‑functional teams and senior leadership Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast‑paced, highly technical environment Strong analytical, data‑driven problem‑solving, and visualization skills Experience leading complex technical and quality issues across multiple organizational levels Familiarity with Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) is a plus Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and schematics, with in‑depth knowledge of electronics and vision system manufacturing processes, standards, and specifications Demonstrated team leadership capability and cross‑functional collaboration experience Strong proficiency in MS Office tools (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook;
