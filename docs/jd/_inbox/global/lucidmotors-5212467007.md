As a Technical Specialist for Chassis Structural Components, you will lead the design and development of components that provide lateral, longitudinal, and vertical vehicle control for world-class electric vehicles. You will collaborate closely with vehicle attribute teams, CAE engineers, suppliers, and system owners to deliver innovative suspension structures from concept through production. &nbsp; &nbsp; Key Responsibilities &nbsp; Lead the design and development of chassis structural components from&nbsp;initial&nbsp;concept through production launch. &nbsp; Define&nbsp;component&nbsp;requirements based on vehicle performance, durability, ride, handling, NVH, and safety targets. &nbsp; Develop technology roadmaps and&nbsp;identify&nbsp;innovative solutions for future vehicle programs. &nbsp; Drive engineering excellence at both the system and&nbsp;component&nbsp;level with exceptional attention to detail. &nbsp; Provide technical leadership, mentorship, and guidance to engineering team members. &nbsp; Work closely with internal stakeholders and external suppliers to achieve&nbsp;optimal&nbsp;system integration. &nbsp; Coordinate virtual engineering activities and physical validation testing to ensure robust designs. &nbsp; Partner with the CAE team to develop and&nbsp;optimize&nbsp;new vehicle architectures and structural concepts. &nbsp; Manage multiple complex projects in a fast-paced, demanding environment. &nbsp; Enhance and&nbsp;maintain&nbsp;foundational engineering documents, including DVP&amp;R, DFMEA, interface diagrams, and boundary diagrams. &nbsp; Review and approve DVP&amp;R documentation, control plans, and other engineering deliverables. &nbsp; Champion&nbsp;the use of AI-driven engineering and simulation tools to reduce dependence on external support and physical testing. &nbsp; Investigate and implement advanced joining and manufacturing technologies. &nbsp; Support technology,&nbsp;supplier, and system selection decisions for future vehicle platforms. &nbsp; Required Qualifications &nbsp; Bachelor's or&nbsp;Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering&nbsp;or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. &nbsp; 8+ years of automotive industry experience, primarily focused on chassis mechanical design. &nbsp; Proven experience designing and releasing components from concept through production. &nbsp; Hands-on experience with prototype assembly, development, and troubleshooting. &nbsp; Experience in technology evaluation, supplier&nbsp;selection, and system architecture development. &nbsp; Strong 3D CAD skills, project leadership experience, and mentoring capabilities. &nbsp; Experience with engineering release processes, change management, and project management. &nbsp; Ability to travel domestically and internationally up to 20% as&nbsp;required. &nbsp; Preferred Qualifications &nbsp; 10+ years of automotive industry experience. &nbsp; Advanced&nbsp;proficiency&nbsp;with CATIA V5 and/or CATIA V6. &nbsp; Strong understanding of vehicle dynamics, NVH, crashworthiness, and chassis performance attributes. &nbsp; Experience performing and teaching CAE analysis methods and simulation tools. &nbsp; Knowledge of automotive mechatronic systems, controls, and integrated chassis technologies. &nbsp; Experience applying AI and data-driven engineering methods to accelerate product development and decision-making. &nbsp; What Success Looks Like &nbsp; Delivers innovative, production-ready chassis structures that meet performance, quality, timing, and cost targets. &nbsp; Serves as the technical authority for&nbsp;chassis&nbsp;structural components within the organization. &nbsp; Drives continuous improvement in engineering processes, simulation methodologies, and AI-enabled development tools. &nbsp; Mentors&nbsp;engineers and&nbsp;elevates&nbsp;the overall technical capability of the chassis engineering team. &nbsp;
