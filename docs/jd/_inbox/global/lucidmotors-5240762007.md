The role is accountable for dyno test integrity, correlation, and productivity. The role operates through disciplined change control, objective launch evidence, cross-functional problem solving. &nbsp; Key Responsibilities &nbsp; • Dyno Operations &amp; Test Cycles • Own dyno stations operations • Maintain dyno stations readiness and safety compliance Performance &amp; Efficiency Mapping • Own efficiency mapping across torque/speed operating envelope • Execute steady-state and transient performance validation Torque, Speed &amp; Power Measurement • Own torque transducer standards, correction factors, and correlation • Ensure speed and power measurement integrity Test Cell Instrumentation • Own instrumentation for temperature, current, voltage, and position • Maintain calibration and traceability of test cell instruments High-Speed Data Acquisition • Own DAQ standards, calibration, and sampling strategies • Ensure data integrity for validation and analytics TPM &amp; Autonomous Maintenance Support • Support TPM deployment across Autonomous Maintenance, Planned Maintenance, and Focused Improvement pillars • Contribute to equipment OEE improvement, MTBF/MTTR reduction, and predictive maintenance strategies • Leverage MES, IIoT, and historian analytics to identify and eliminate losses HV Safety &amp; Compliance • Enforce HV LOTO, HVIL continuity, and mandatory HV PPE for all work on or near EV Powertrain equipment • Comply with SASO, Saudi MOL, IEC 60204, and functional safety standards (ISO 13849 / IEC 62061) • Support HV emergency response including thermal runaway isolation and battery-related incident procedures • Achieve and maintain HV safety competency appropriate to scope of work Launch Governance &amp; Ramp-Up • Support AMP-2 Safe Launch activities, run-at-rate events, and capacity verification within scope • Contribute to Launch Readiness Reviews, Layered Process Audits, and Process Confirmation routines • Participate in QRQC / RCCA escalations during launch and ramp-up phases Dynamometer Engineering &amp; Test Integrity • Commission and sustain dyno controls, inverter/battery simulators, cooling, oil conditioning, torque/speed measurement, DAQ, safety systems and facility interfaces. • Develop automated test sequences and, where applicable, LabVIEW or equivalent softwares; manage recipes, configuration and software release. • Correlate torque, speed, power, temperature, voltage/current and efficiency results across cells using reference units and uncertainty analysis. • Own cell availability, preventive maintenance, instrumentation health, test throughput and safe response to HV, overspeed, thermal, fluid and rotating-equipment hazards. Launch Governance, Change Control &amp; Operational Readiness • Support FAT/SAT, commissioning, equipment acceptance, Safe Launch, run-at-rate and capacity verification with objective evidence and disciplined punch-list closure. • Control production, process, software and test changes through documented risk assessment, approvals, validation, revision history and rollback or containment planning. • Participate in readiness reviews, Layered Process Audits, Process Confirmation and QRQC/RCCA; close assigned actions with verified effectiveness. Safety, Quality, Data Integrity &amp; Compliance • Apply role-specific HV LOTO/HVIL, machine safety, stored-energy isolation, electrical safe-work and PPE requirements; never bypass safety devices or product protections. • Protect product genealogy, calibrated-tool status, test/measurement integrity, document control and audit-ready objective evidence. • Comply with applicable AMP-2, SASO, Saudi labor/EHS, IATF 16949, IEC 60204 and functional-safety requirements within assigned scope. Saudization, Capability Transfer &amp; Collaboration • Build Saudi national capability through structured OJT, ILU/skills matrices, practical assessment, certification, cross-training and documented knowledge transfer. • Create technical standards, troubleshooting guides and training material; support interviews and KSA university/TVTC engagement where requested. • Collaborate across Production, Quality, Maintenance, Automation/Robotics, Manufacturing Engineering, EHS, IT/OT, MES, Supply Chain, Product Engineering and suppliers. &nbsp; Skills • Deep dynamometer test expertise • Efficiency mapping and performance validation fluency • Torque/speed/power measurement expertise • Data-analytics capability • Effective cross-functional communicator • Technical standards, structured problem solving, risk-based decision making and controlled documentation • Data literacy: KPI definition, trend analysis, evidence-based validation and clear executive/shop-floor communication • Cross-functional influence, supplier coordination and disciplined ownership in a greenfield launch environment • Coaching, competency assessment and knowledge transfer in a multicultural English/Arabic workplace &nbsp;
