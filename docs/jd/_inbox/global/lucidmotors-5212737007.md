Position Overview &nbsp; The Senior Systems Engineer leads end-of-line and in-process test capability for high-voltage battery packs at AMP-2 in KSA. This hands-on role owns test-process definition, launch support, issue investigation, and continuous improvement across production. The engineer will partner with local manufacturing, process, and quality teams and collaborate closely with US-based battery design, systems, software, and quality organizations to ensure safe, reliable, high-quality battery production. &nbsp; Key Responsibilities &nbsp; Lead the definition, implementation, and debugging of end-of-line test processes for high-voltage battery packs at AMP-2 &nbsp; Perform battery design and process failure mode analysis to continuously improve the test process and capture newly identified failure modes &nbsp; Perform integration validation and testing for updated hardware and software revisions to ensure product quality &nbsp; Work closely with manufacturing, process, and quality teams to ensure engineering requirements are properly met &nbsp; Lead root-cause analysis using fishbone and 8D, driving issues to closure and preventing production interruptions &nbsp; Perform fleet data analysis to continuously improve end-of-line tests detection and minimize field failures &nbsp; Provide timely feedback to the US engineering team on project progress, risks, and technical challenges &nbsp; Develop on-site diagnostic and analysis capability in KSA to enable advanced investigations and root-cause analysis &nbsp; Develop analytics dashboards in collaboration with the data science teams and enable reliable tracking of quality indicators such as FPY and failure causes &nbsp; Skills &nbsp; Hands-on experience working on high-voltage systems and deep understanding of the principles of high-voltage safety &nbsp; Experience working in prototype and mass manufacturing environments supporting creation and implementation of test specifications for end-of-line testing &nbsp; Deep understanding of battery quality KPIs such as capacity, DCIR, and contact resistances &nbsp; Proficiency with large-scale data analysis tools like Python, SQL, or MATLAB and CAN data analysis tools such as CANalyzer/ CANape &nbsp; Strong problem-solving skills using methodologies such as fishbone and 8D &nbsp; Strong understanding of battery architecture and components such as contactors, HV chain, BMS fundamentals, and power electronics &nbsp; Excellent technical English is a must , both spoken and written, with the ability to communicate complex engineering concepts clearly and precisely &nbsp; Willingness to travel to Lucid HQ in California as needed &nbsp; Minimum Qualifications &nbsp; Bachelor's degree in electrical, mechanical, or a related technical field &nbsp; 5+ years’ experience in the field of high voltage battery systems, BMS, power electronics, or similar automotive engineering roles &nbsp;
