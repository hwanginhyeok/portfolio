About Lucid At Lucid, we are creating exceptional mobility experiences through innovation to drive the world forward. Built on Lucid’s proprietary technology and software-defined vehicle architecture, our award-winning vehicles bring our “Compromise Nothing™” approach to the global automotive market. That means refusing to choose between performance and sustainability, design and engineering, ambition and integrity. In Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity, we have designed and built vehicles that have redefined their segments, combining exceptional range, performance, design, and expansive space in a single experience. &nbsp; We achieve this through deep vertical integration, with design, engineering, and production&nbsp;happening in-house&nbsp;across our global offices and&nbsp;manufacturing&nbsp;facilities.&nbsp;Our&nbsp;teams&nbsp;come&nbsp;from&nbsp;industries&nbsp;around the world, united by a shared commitment to excellence.&nbsp;By refusing to settle, you&nbsp;can&nbsp;help&nbsp;redefine&nbsp;what’s&nbsp;possible and&nbsp;shape the future of mobility. As a&nbsp;Senior Quality Engineer, ADAS, you will lead all Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) Quality Engineering activities at AMP2 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA). You will thrive in a fast‑paced, highly complex manufacturing environment, working alongside a motivated, cross‑functional team supporting cutting‑edge vehicle programs. In this role, you will partner across the full product lifecycle to proactively protect Lucid’s production lines from quality risks. You will collaborate closely with Supplier Quality, Quality Engineering, Manufacturing, Product Engineering, Launch, and Field Quality teams to ensure robust quality execution from development through launch and production. &nbsp; You Will Ensure compliance of quality deliverables at each project milestone during the launch phase in alignment with corporate standards Lead quality test and validation follow‑up with Product Engineering to ensure requirements are met Review, validate, and support APQP/PPAP activities in partnership with Supplier Quality and Product Engineering Ensure part maturity and readiness to secure production line stability Monitor responsiveness with Incoming Quality and suppliers on non‑conforming or defective parts Define Incoming Quality Inspection requirements and support training for internal teams and third‑party resources Collect, track, and resolve Incoming Quality findings during launch to protect production continuity and address issues with suppliers and cross‑functional teams Proactively identify supplier risks and lead mitigation actions in collaboration with Supplier Quality Development Drive problem‑solving activities with the Launch team; validate implementation and effectiveness of solutions through Gemba and ensure long‑term sustainability Lead containment and root‑cause resolution for internal quality issues identified through Pre‑Delivery Inspection (PDI) and Lucid Customer Product Audit findings Improve customer experience by leading 0MiS, 3MiS, 6MiS, and similar quality findings, including containment and corrective actions, in coordination with Field Quality, Supplier Quality, Product Engineering, and Manufacturing Ensure readiness and approvals for New Parts Introduction (NPI), including trials, parts change management, and PPAP approvals Monitor and track Engineering Change Requests (ECRs) within PLM or equivalent systems You Bring Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering, Quality Engineering, Total Quality Management, or a related field 5+ years of relevant professional experience (post-graduation) 5+ years of experience within a greenfield OEM environment (required) Minimum of 3 years of experience in a similar ADAS or senior quality engineering role (required) Strong verbal, written, and presentation skills Proven ability to influence, motivate, and build alignment across cross‑functional teams and senior leadership Ability to operate effectively in a fast‑paced environment with multiple simultaneous priorities Strong data analysis and visualization skills Demonstrated capability to lead complex quality issues and communicate effectively at all levels of the organization Familiarity with Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) is a plus Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and schematics, with in‑depth knowledge of ADAS standards and specifications Compensation &amp;
