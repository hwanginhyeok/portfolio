System Integration Engineer 🛠️ About our Systems Integration Team Wayve's Systems Integration team brings together hardware and software across autonomous vehicle platforms, development benches and Hardware-in-the-Loop systems. The team prototypes, assembles, brings up and troubleshoots complex systems to improve the performance, reliability and repeatability of our next-generation vehicle platforms. 🧠 Your day-to-day As a System Integration Engineer at Wayve, you'll lead the integration and bring-up of cutting-edge autonomous vehicle platforms. You'll work across hardware and software boundaries to prototype, assemble and test complex systems both in-vehicle and on development benches, while supporting vehicle integration development and issue troubleshooting to improve the performance and reliability of our next-generation systems. 🧩 What you’ll be working on: • Integrate and bring up autonomous systems on internal and partner vehicles. • Architect, prototype and evaluate new hardware systems. • Support fleet troubleshooting and issue resolution. • Inform and improve system integration processes and workflows. • Bring up and maintain development benches and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) systems. 🙌 You should apply if: • You have 7+ years of experience integrating and developing complex systems. • You bring broad technical comprehension across electrical, mechanical and software systems. • You have proven hands-on experience debugging and assembling hardware/software systems. • You communicate and collaborate effectively across engineering disciplines. • You bring multidisciplinary troubleshooting skills, a strong sense of ownership and the initiative to drive issues through to resolution. • You have a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or equivalent experience. Nice to have • You have experience working on autonomous vehicles, robotics or similar advanced systems. • You are skilled in prototyping electronics, wire harnesses and custom hardware components. • You are comfortable working in Linux environments and with scripting tools. • You can read and understand code in languages such as C++, Python or JavaScript. • You have experience using application-level software for testing, flashing and triaging issues. • You have experience working with suppliers and contract manufacturers on design validation and manufacturing.
