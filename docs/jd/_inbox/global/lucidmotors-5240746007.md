The Maintenance Manager, Powertrain leads 24/7 Maintenance and Reliability execution across AMP-2 Drive Unit and HV Battery shops, building the organization, CMMS, PM/PdM system, AM strategy, spares strategy, shift model and technical Saudization development pipeline from commissioning through volume production. Mission: Deliver safe, capable, launch ready and sustainable Powertrain operations with local technical capability. Key Responsibilities: Maintenance operating system Own safe 24/7 response, shift coverage, escalation, tier routines and maintenance handover. Deliver uptime, MTBF, MTTR, PM compliance, schedule compliance, backlog and cost performance. Establish standard breakdown, emergency-work, defect-elimination and return-to-service governance. Deliver AM strategy Reliability &amp; TPM Deploy asset criticality, RCM, PM optimization, CBM/PdM and chronic-loss elimination with Reliability Engineering. Own RCA quality and countermeasure verification for high-impact and repeat failures. Partner with Production on AM Levels 1–3, CIL, centerlines and operator equipment care. CMMS, planning &amp; spares Own CMMS governance, asset/BOM/task-list, planning discipline and shutdown readiness. Own critical-spares, repairables, obsolescence, vendor lead time and inventory-health strategy. People, budget &amp; launch Build and develop supervisors, engineers, planners, team leads and technicians; define competency/certification architecture. Own Opex, spare Capex, service contracts and supplier performance. Own maintenance readiness for FAT/SAT, safe launch, run-at-rate, capacity verification and future variants. Enterprise, launch &amp; workforce expectations Safety: zero bypass of LOTO, guarding, interlocks, HV controls or approved work instructions. Quality: protect product conformity, traceability and calibrated-tool discipline; contain abnormalities and preserve objective evidence. Delivery: support stable flow, takt/cycle achievement and rapid, structured escalation of constraints. Cost: reduce scrap, rework, downtime, energy and consumable losses through data-based countermeasures. People: build Saudi national capability through coaching, cross-training and knowledge transfer. Continuous improvement: participate in tier meetings, 5S, Kaizen, QRQC and closed-loop corrective action. Launch: Interfaces with Production, Quality, Maintenance, Automation/Robotics, Manufacturing Engineering, EHS, Supply Chain, IT/OT, HR/L&amp;D and equipment suppliers as applicable. Skills: Maintenance/reliability leadership: RCM, PM optimization, CBM/PdM, RCA, AM, etc High-automation production equipment and CMMS governance Organization design, shift execution and technical talent development Budget, contracts and supplier management Executive communication and crisis leadership Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s in mechanical, electrical or mechatronics engineering 10+ years in industrial maintenance/reliability with 4+ years leading functions High-automation automotive/EV and greenfield-launch experience Strong CMMS/TPM record English fluency Experience developing local technical talent in a multicultural workforce Must be able to work around automated equipment and, where authorized, high-voltage Powertrain products.
