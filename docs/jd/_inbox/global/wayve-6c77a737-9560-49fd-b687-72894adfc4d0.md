## JD 본문

Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Driving Product Teams The Driving Product team builds the AI driver's core behaviours, taking models from data and training through evaluation and on-road validation, then shipping them into the car on timelines measured in months, not years. The team's work has direct impact on high-priority commercial deliveries, including Nissan production and Robotaxi, and spans the full spectrum from Level 2 to Level 4 driving. It's a small, high-ownership team at a point of rapid growth, blending ML research with shipping-focused engineering. 🧠 Your day-to-day - Develop the AI driver model architecture and training algorithms to introduce and enhance driving behaviours for L2/L3/L4 - Own key parts of the training model lifecycle, including evaluation strategy, success metrics, and iteration planning - Mine, bucket, and curate real-world and synthetic data to teach specific driving behaviours, and implement data schemes to support training - Run and analyse on-road and offline experiments, translate results into clear next steps, and drive improvements through repeated training cycles 🧩 What you'll be working on: - Models that predict vehicle trajectories and interact directly with controllers and actuators, closing the loop between prediction and real-world vehicle performance - End-to-end work across data, model training, evaluation, deployment, and performance monitoring - On-road and off-road metrics that diagnose issues and drive data-led model iterations - Production-ready ML systems built with strong software engineering, quality, and reliability practices - Methods spanning reinforcement learning, behavioural cloning, and distillation, working with multi-sensor inputs such as camera, radar, and lidar where relevant 👤 You should apply if: - Proven track record training deep learning models with clear end-to-end ownership: data, training, evaluation, and iteration - You've taken ML models into production, working through real-world constraints, quality, and safety requirements - Strong Python skills and hands-on experience with modern ML frameworks - Comfortable shipping production-facing ML, not just research or proof-of-concept work - Background in either end-to-end autonomous driving/embodied AI, or large-scale product ML systems such as recommendations or ranking - Bring reinforcement learning or behavioural cloning experience, especially where it materially improved real-world performance - Familiar with model compression and deploying ML to embedded systems (nice to have) 🌱 Not ticking every box? That's totally okay! If you're passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every requirement. More about Wayve: 🚗 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end to end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale. Instead of relying on hand coded rules or pre mapped environments, our AI Driver learns to drive by understanding the world around it. The result is technology that navigates complex urban environments with intelligence, precision and natural flow, unlocking meaningful advances in both safety and efficiency. We believe autonomy represents a once in a generation transformation in how people and goods move, comparable to the shift from horses to cars, and from human driven vehicles to intelligent machines. Our ambition is to make autonomy universal. Wayve's mapless and hardware agnostic AI platform integrates with global OEM partners, enabling continuous software evolution and unlocking advanced levels of automation from L2 plus through to L4 as our core AI model scales. In a race increasingly defined by intelligence and real world learning, Wayve is taking a distinct approach, building a generalisable driving intelligence that can power any vehicle, anywhere. By combining embodied AI with scalable deployment, we are creating technology that can be shaped to each OEM brand and driver experience, accelerating the transition to a safer, more intelligent future of mobility. How we work 💻 — Locations & Flexible Working: Our main hubs are in London, Sunnyvale, Yokohama, Herzliya, Vancouver and Leonberg.
