The Role: Collaborate with Cross-Engineering team in design, prototyping, validation, and production of Service tools. Work with vendors and suppliers to efficiently turn prototypes into production tools for the Service environment. Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to develop, maintain, and improve diagnostic and service solutions for vehicle systems. Support the creation, review, and validation of fault trees, diagnostic workflows, troubleshooting guides, and service procedures. Take projects from idea to finished product as smoothly and quickly as possible. Adapt quickly to changing priorities and contribute effectively within a fast-paced engineering environment. Perform under time constraints to produce excellent results Expectations: 2+ years of Automotive Experience in automotive diagnostics, troubleshooting, validation, systems engineering, or a related technical field. Ability to identify service and diagnostic needs and translate requirements into practical tooling solutions for field and production environments. Ability to independently perform testing, root cause analysis, validation, and issue resolution in a fast-paced environment. Experience in vehicle communication networks including CAN, LIN, Automotive Ethernet, and diagnostic protocols. Ability to analyze complex vehicle systems and translate technical issues into structured diagnostic workflows and fault trees. Experience supporting the creation, review, validation, and continuous improvement of diagnostic procedures and troubleshooting content. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Willingness to travel as needed to support testing, validation, implementation, or training activities. Advantageous: Familiarity with engineering processes, testing methodologies, and validation activities. 2+ years of relevant experience in vehicle diagnostics. Experience developing procedures, work instructions, or training materials. Experience supporting field operations, service centers, mobile service teams, or body shop networks. Proficient in Jira, Jama, 3DEXPERIENCE (3DX), and other engineering lifecycle management tools. Experience working with high-voltage (HV) vehicle systems, electric vehicle architectures, or autonomous vehicle technologies. Education Requirements: Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, or Automotive Technology.
