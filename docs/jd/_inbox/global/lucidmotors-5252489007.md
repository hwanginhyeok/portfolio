Technical Specialist , Steering Systems Controls and Integration &nbsp; As a Technical Specialist in Steering Systems Controls &amp; Electronics, you will develop, build, calibrate and validate the systems responsible for Chassis and Vehicle Dynamics control. You will work closely with teams across the engineering group to develop world-class vehicle dynamics-shaping functions from concept to production. &nbsp; &nbsp; The Role: Develop and maintain the requirements and specifications for electric power steering control systems from initial concept to SOP for multiple vehicle programs &nbsp; Work with internal teams such as Vehicle Controls, Vehicle Dynamics and ADAS, as well as external suppliers, to design, build, deliver, test and calibrate prototype software in virtual, HiL and vehicle environments&nbsp; &nbsp; Create, execute and document system level design validation plans including virtual, HiL and vehicle-based activity to take your concepts and prototype software to customer readiness &nbsp; Work across multiple complex projects in a demanding environment &nbsp; Travel domestically and internationally as required &nbsp; &nbsp; Qualifications: &nbsp; 7+ years of automotive experience &nbsp; Strong experience in development and delivery of steering control systems &nbsp; Experience with Vector tools (CANape, CANalyzer, CDMStudio, etc.) &nbsp; Strong understanding of Vehicle Dynamics theory &nbsp; Hands-on practical experience in prototype development &nbsp; Experience with JIRA, Confluence and requirements management tools &nbsp; Familiarity with software development (Matlab/Python/CAPL scripting, model based)&nbsp; &nbsp; Ability to read and work with electrical schematics, with sound understanding of electrical communication principles &nbsp; &nbsp; Advantageous: &nbsp; &nbsp; Excellent problem-solving ability, with an ability to use fundamental principles and data-driven action &nbsp; Desire for continual learning &nbsp; Driven-vehicle attributes development experience is a plus &nbsp; Eager and strong fundamentals-based problem-solving approaches &nbsp; &nbsp; Education: &nbsp; Bachelor's degree in a relevant Engineering or related technical discipline is required.&nbsp; Master's degree or higher education is preferred.&nbsp;
