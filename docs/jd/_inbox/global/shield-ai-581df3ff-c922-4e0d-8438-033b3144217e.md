JOB DESCRIPTION As an Embedded Software Engineer at Shield AI, you will develop, deploy, and optimize embedded software for advanced autonomous systems operating in real-world environments. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver robust, scalable solutions that push the boundaries of intelligent systems for defense and security applications. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Design, implement, test, and optimize embedded software for real-time autonomous systems on resource-constrained hardware platforms - Build reliable, maintainable, and high-performance code, primarily in C/C++ - Develop comprehensive tests and evaluate performance in simulation and on hardware - Diagnose and debug complex embedded software issues, including concurrency and integration problems - Work closely with hardware, autonomy, and robotics teams to ensure seamless end-to-end system integration - Contribute to system and software architecture design, technical documentation, and code reviews - Take new features from ideation through verification, refinement, and deployment into active field operations - Stay current with the latest advancements in embedded systems, autonomy, and robotics REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS - Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience - Strong professional experience developing and debugging embedded software in C/C++ - Experience with real-time operating systems (RTOS) or embedded Linux - Familiarity with embedded software for robotics, autonomous systems, or safety-critical applications - Strong understanding of software engineering best practices, including testing, debugging, version control, and maintainable design - Experience developing and debugging software on Linux - Ability to communicate technical decisions and collaborate effectively across disciplines PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS - Experience with microcontrollers, SoCs, FPGA-based systems or sensor integration - Experience in safety-critical or fielded embedded software for autonomy, robotics, or aerospace platforms - Understanding of connectivity protocols (e.g., UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, Ethernet) - Experience in system-level validation: software- or hardware-in-the-loop simulation or direct field testing - Contributions to open-source embedded projects or relevant applied research BENEFITS - Competitive pay + Bonus + Equity -
