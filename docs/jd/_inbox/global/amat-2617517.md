The Role Applied Materials is growing at a pace that is driving significant increases in headcount and facilities. As a result, we expect over the next five years to have the most exciting and active project pipeline in our history. In addition to enabling this growth, Applied Materials is investing significantly in labs to augment the R&D capability in Korea. The Global Workplace and Infrastructure (GWI) team is responsible for all facilities, from acquisition through operation to disposition. The Labs are mission critical to our success and they operate 24x7 365 days a year. As a member of the GWI team, this position will play an exciting and important role in our success. The Korea Lab Facilities Engineer will be an SME in one or more of - Electrical, Mechanical, Gas & Chemical Engineering with hands-on experience in Semiconductor manufacturing either a Fab or AT site. Key Responsibilities Overall Infrastructure Management: Be part of the design, installation, operation, and maintenance of Electrical/Mechanical/Chemical infrastructure to support semiconductor processing in the Lab. Provides design engineering support for base build projects, system expansion, modifications, and new equipment installations by developing the statement of work, sequence of operations and basis of design. This includes providing design input and submittal review. Responsible for driving global alignment, reliability and efficiency improvements, cost reductions, capacity planning, improved automation and monitoring, regulatory compliance, cost reductions and alignment to sustainability goals. Prioritization - execute short- and long-range planning efforts to maintain modern and efficient buildings through a proactive Capital plan. Build: Assist the GWI Head of Korea in Building to the Plan – this includes: All Retrofit, EOL and Eng. projects within the lab, this would also include tool hook-ups to facilities utility infrastructure (Power, CDA/OFA, PCW, Gases, Chemicals, Pumps, Scrubbers, Abatements, etc.,), this would involve working closely with the business tool owner to understand the tools and the process they intend to perform, plan for those tool hook-ups, agree on SLA’s upfront. Identify business requirements and feedback by effective engagement, meet business needs through effective cross functional collaboration with GIS / IT, Security, Procurement, HR, EHS, Legal and vendors leadership and teams. Develop Program and manage life cycle refresh, renovation, maintenance and repairs, energy and utility management to contribute to Company’s carbon footprint goals. Develop, review, and maintain Applied Global Best Known Methods (BKM) documents with focus and emphasis on Operations and Maintenance Reliability and/or Performance. Compliance and Safety: Ensure all Electrical, Mechanical, Gas and Chemical systems comply with industry standards, safety regulations, local state and central Government stipulations and company policies.
