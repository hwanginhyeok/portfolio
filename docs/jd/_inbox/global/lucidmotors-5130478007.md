We are seeking a Sr. Motion Planning and Controls Engineer, Autonomous Driving to design, develop, and deploy industry-leading planning and control algorithms for next-generation driver assistance and autonomous systems.&nbsp; &nbsp; This position is based in Newark, CA and requires onsite presence five days a week. &nbsp; &nbsp; The Role: &nbsp; &nbsp; As a Sr. Motion Planning and Controls Engineer, you will play a key role in developing high-performance motion planning and control features for Lucid’s ADAS and autonomous driving systems. You’ll work across software, hardware, and systems teams to ensure robust design, testing, and deployment of production-grade lateral and longitudinal control algorithms.&nbsp; &nbsp; Responsibilities: &nbsp; &nbsp; Design, prototype, test, and deploy motion planning and control algorithms for ADAS and autonomous driving systems.&nbsp; &nbsp; Implement state-of-the-art approaches to trajectory planning, route planning, and real-time control.&nbsp; &nbsp; Develop and tune classical and modern controllers (e.g., LQR, MPC, PID, Adaptive, Robust) for both lateral and longitudinal vehicle dynamics.&nbsp; &nbsp; Conduct model-based design and validation using MATLAB/Simulink or equivalent tools.&nbsp; &nbsp; Participate in the full development cycle, from simulation and SIL/HIL testing to in-vehicle implementation and release.&nbsp; &nbsp; Collaborate closely with systems, validation, perception, and platform teams to ensure safe and seamless software integration.&nbsp; &nbsp; Contribute to architectural decisions, safety assessments, and product scalability.&nbsp; &nbsp; Required Qualifications: &nbsp; &nbsp; Bachelor’s degree in Automotive, Aerospace, Mechanical Engineering or a related field (advanced degrees preferred).&nbsp; &nbsp; 5+ years of experience (with MS) or 3+ years (with PhD) in motion control or trajectory planning for ADAS/Autonomous Driving.&nbsp; &nbsp; Deep knowledge of control system design and real-time implementation using modern control approaches (e.g., LQR, MPC).&nbsp; &nbsp; Proven experience deploying lateral and longitudinal controllers in a production or research vehicle environment.&nbsp; &nbsp; Proficiency in modeling and simulation tools (e.g., MATLAB/Simulink).&nbsp; &nbsp; Solid understanding of ADAS/AD systems, robotics, and vehicle dynamics.&nbsp; &nbsp; Strong communication skills and ability to thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.&nbsp; &nbsp; Preferred Qualifications: &nbsp; &nbsp; Proficiency in programming languages such as C++, Python, and MATLAB.&nbsp; &nbsp; Experience with middleware frameworks like ROS.&nbsp; &nbsp; Familiarity with AUTOSAR and embedded software development practices.&nbsp; &nbsp; Experience in bench testing, SIL/HIL integration, and scenario-based simulation.&nbsp; &nbsp; Knowledge of safety standards and automotive system regulations (e.g., ISO 26262).&nbsp; &nbsp; Background in agile development environments, version control (e.g., Git), and static code analysis. &nbsp;
