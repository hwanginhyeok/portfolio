We are seeking an experienced Senior Process Development Engineer to lead the specification and advancement of powertrain product, process, and equipment technologies at Lucid Motors. This role requires a strong foundation in mechanical design, feasibility testing, electromechanical equipment integration, and process validation, including DQ, IQ, OQ, and PQ. The ideal candidate will bring hands-on experience in laser welding, equipment design, product design for manufacturability, and metrology-based quality evaluation. Success in this position requires the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment while maintaining a strong commitment to technical excellence and continuous improvement. &nbsp; &nbsp; The Role: &nbsp; Lead new product development efforts, including ideation, feasibility studies, and prototype development. &nbsp; Develop workholding strategies and solutions to adapt universal capital equipment for process and metrology requirements. &nbsp; Collaborate cross-functionally to define and refine new product performance requirements and specifications. &nbsp; Perform materials characterization, analysis, and selection; partner with modeling engineers to develop technical roadmaps and execution plans. &nbsp; Work closely with process engineers to define product-specific process and equipment requirements, supporting a smooth handoff to Operations, including training and implementation. &nbsp; Contribute to the development of test methods and validation plans to evaluate powertrain product performance. &nbsp; Design and execute experiments with vendors, OEMs, and system integrators to optimize product, process, and equipment performance. &nbsp; Support&nbsp;the&nbsp;equipment commissioning including FAT and SAT activities &nbsp; Lead technical discussions with suppliers to explore novel design and manufacturing solutions. &nbsp; Train and support technicians in equipment and process operations for prototyping, feasibility testing, and product/process/equipment validation. &nbsp; &nbsp; Qualifications: &nbsp; Product design and process development engineering experience. &nbsp; 2D drawing and 3D CAD proficiency required. &nbsp; Strong documentation, communication, and collaboration skills. &nbsp; Knowledge of advanced manufacturing processes, including laser welding, marking, cutting, wire bonding, and smart welding. &nbsp; Familiarity with DOE and validation methods, including DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ, window testing, Cpk, and process capability studies. &nbsp; Ability to travel periodically to Lucid AMP locations for onboarding, training, testing, and validation. &nbsp; &nbsp; Preferred: &nbsp; 5+ years of product design or manufacturing engineering experience. &nbsp; Experience refining products through metrology and testing. &nbsp; Experience with SPC monitoring for production processes and equipment. &nbsp; Experience developing workholding solutions for manufacturing and lab testing. &nbsp;
