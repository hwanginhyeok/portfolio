The ideal candidate brings strong enterprise sales and business development experience, with the ability to create market demand, build direct relationships with end customers, and leverage local partners to develop and close new business opportunities. Develop and manage a local partner ecosystem, including distributors, resellers, SIs, and technology partners, to expand market reach and accelerate pipeline generation.
