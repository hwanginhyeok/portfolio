Position Summary As part of Applied Materials’ centralized Employee Relations (ER) organization, this role partners closely with Global Employment Law (GEL), HR Business Partners (HRBPs), Ethics & Compliance, Security, and business leaders to manage employee relations matters and workplace investigations across the Asia region. This position is designed as a regional APAC Employee Relations consulting role based in Korea, supporting Korea and additional Asia countries depending on business and organizational needs. The role may provide coverage across markets including China, Taiwan and Japan as part of a centralized regional ER support model. The consultant will serve as a trusted advisor on employee performance, conduct, misconduct, investigations, and performance management matters, ensuring fair, consistent, and compliant employee treatment aligned with company values, policies, and local labor regulations. This role offers the opportunity to build deep APAC Employee Relations expertise within a highly collaborative global ER & Compliance structure, directly reporting to the Global Head of HR ER & Compliance Key Responsibilities Employee Relations & Investigations Manage end-to-end Employee Relations investigations from intake through interviews, analysis, escalation, redirection, and closure, maintaining accurate and timely documentation. Handle complex or escalated matters including allegations of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, misconduct, and other serious policy concerns. Gather and analyze relevant data to support findings and recommendations related to repeated or serious policy violations. Provide guidance on investigation outcomes, including review and recommendation of corrective actions up to and including termination, consistent with company values and behaviors. Performance & Conduct Management Serve as a trusted advisor to managers on policy interpretation, discipline, misconduct, and performance management. Manage and support formal performance management processes. Assist managers with drafting and editing performance management and disciplinary documentation. Partner with managers during annual review cycles to ensure alignment with local compliance standards and company expectations. Consultation, Partnership & Coaching Provide advice and counsel to HRBPs to support effective resolution of employee and manager issues. Deliver strategic employee relations and performance management consultation to leaders across multiple countries and functions. Act as a liaison between HR COEs, GEL, HRBPs, and non-HR partners to appropriately escalate or redirect concerns. Partner effectively across regional and global stakeholders within a highly matrixed organization. Data, Metrics & Continuous Improvement Track, analyze, and report on ER case metrics including resolution outcomes, trends, timeliness, and customer satisfaction. Identify organizational trends and root-cause issues impacting employee relations and organizational health. Contribute to and lead continuous improvement initiatives to enhance ER processes, policies, tools, and technologies across the APAC region. Training & Enablement In partnership with GEL, provide training, technical support, and guidance to HR and managers on Employee Relations and performance management processes. Proactively assess manager and team development needs and recommend or implement appropriate training solutions and tools.
