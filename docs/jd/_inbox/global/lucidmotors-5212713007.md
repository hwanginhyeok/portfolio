multi-variable problems within Drive Unit System in close collaboration with cross-functional teams Empower the team to solve complex component/system level problems using standard problem-solving techniques and Design of Experiments (DoE) Enforce data driven proactive problem solving, root cause analysis &amp; issue avoidance due to process deviations or critical part quality issues Establish technical depth in Electric Drive Unit hardware &amp; its interface with other systems within the team Create an environment of trust &amp; efficient work culture within the team to deliver impactful results Manage conflicts/escalation from several functions in the plant with competing priorities Develop organizational processes and tools to improve productivity &amp; other cross-functional teams Skills Experience in using DFMEA, PFMEA, DVP&amp;R, DFM, &amp; RCA with cross-functional teams Prior experience in leading people &amp; working in manufacturing environment Engineering-in-Training (EIT) or certification in engineering fundamentals Six Sigma Black Belt Certification RedX apprentice/Journeyman certification Minimum Qualifications Bachelor of Science degree in Mechanical or Automotive Engineering with 10+ years of automotive experience in design and release of vehicle attributes, or Master of Science degree with 8+ years of automotive experience Experience in product problem solving using 8D, 7 Step, RedX, DMAIC or similar methodologies Experience in setting clear KPIs &amp;
