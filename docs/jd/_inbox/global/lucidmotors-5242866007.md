Report to Test and Validation team and will be responsible for: &nbsp; Conduct electrical test and validation for automotive ECU modules per approved test plans and internal specifications – LV124 &nbsp; Configure and operate laboratory equipment such as power supplies, oscilloscopes, current probes, and electronic loads &nbsp; Document test conditions, results, and observations to support DV/PV reporting &nbsp; Organize waveform data, plots, and test artifacts for engineering review &nbsp; Work closely with LVEE engineers to ensure coverage, schedule adherence, and safe lab operations &nbsp; Create test plans to validate electrical performance of various electrical components, including E ‑ fuses, voltage regulators, microcontroller power stages, and other ECU ‑ level circuitry. &nbsp; Conduct environmental and mechanical tests in support of Lucid Engineering team. &nbsp; &nbsp; Qualifications: &nbsp; Understanding of automotive electrical systems and lab testing best practices &nbsp; Extensive experience with oscilloscopes, power supplies, and current measurement techniques &nbsp; Strong skills working with Microsoft Word and Excel for data organization, analysis and report generation. &nbsp; A strong desire to create state-of-the-art engineering products as an integral part of a capable team. &nbsp; Ability to review and provide input in design reviews for PCBA schematics and layouts &nbsp; Experience with Confluence and JIRA &nbsp; Excellent communication skills in both technical and non-technical environments. &nbsp; Strong attention to detail and willingness to work hands on in a validation lab environment &nbsp; &nbsp; Preferred: &nbsp; Prior experience running electrical validation for Automotive ECU’s. &nbsp; Experience with NI Test Stand/LabView is a plus &nbsp; &nbsp; Education Requirements: &nbsp; Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering and 5-10 years of relevant experience&nbsp;
