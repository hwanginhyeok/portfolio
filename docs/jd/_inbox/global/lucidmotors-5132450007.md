Managing every aspect of design engineering from concept to production and beyond.&nbsp; &nbsp; Develop system-level solutions that balance electrical performance, mechanical robustness, manufacturability, cost, serviceability, and customer experience. &nbsp; Lead supplier technical reviews, DFMEA reviews, tooling reviews, validation alignment, and production launch activities. &nbsp; Prototype &amp; Test : Design, build, and test your prototypes &nbsp; Define and support executing environmental, vibration, thermal shock, temperature cycling, ingress protection, durability, and mechanical qualification testing &nbsp; Drive design changes based on test results and real-world feedback. &nbsp; Innovate &amp; Optimize : Continuously optimize designs for cost and performance without sacrificing safety or reliability. &nbsp; Collaborate : Work closely with electrical engineers, quality assurance, manufacturing, and other cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration of mechanical and electrical systems. &nbsp; Mentor : Provide technical guidance to junior engineers, lead internal design reviews, and drive engineering decisions through analysis, testing, and first-principles reasoning. &nbsp; Continuously Learn : Embrace new challenges and expand your engineering skills in a fast-paced, dynamic environment. &nbsp; &nbsp; Qualifications: &nbsp; Ideally 3-6 years of relevant work experience but open to all levels of experience if paired with strong technical proficiency &nbsp; Rock-solid Mechanical Engineering fundamentals and a flair for creative problem-solving &nbsp; Experience designing and packaging vehicle wiring harnesses with consideration for routing, retention, serviceability, EMC, thermal environments, crash requirements, and manufacturing constraints. &nbsp; Strong 3D CAD skills, proficient with CATIA or similar software &nbsp; Proven Design for Manufacturing (DFM) and Design for Assembly (DFA) experience across injection molded plastics, sheet metal, die castings, busbars, and adhesives. &nbsp; Experience with Root Cause Analysis methods, such as 8D &nbsp; Preferred: &nbsp; Experience with automotive engineering &nbsp; Experience with battery disconnect units, HV junction boxes, and vehicle power distribution architectures &nbsp; Strong understanding of high-voltage design principles including creepage and clearance, touch-safe architectures, HVIL systems, shielding, grounding, insulation systems, and electrical safety requirements. &nbsp;
