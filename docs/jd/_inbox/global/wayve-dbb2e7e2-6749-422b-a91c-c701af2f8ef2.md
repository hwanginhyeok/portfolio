## JD 본문

Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Technical Program Management Teams The Technical Program Management team sits within Engineering Operations & Technical Program Management. Our TPMs are force multipliers, helping teams move faster, more effectively and with purpose. In Japan, you’ll be the only TPM on the ground, bridging Wayve’s global R&D with local delivery. 🧠 Your day-to-day As a Technical Program Manager for Robotaxi, you’ll own the programs that bring our robotaxi service to life, from vehicle and fleet readiness to on-the-ground launch and scaling in market. Based in Yokohama, you’ll work across autonomy, vehicle software, hardware, operations, safety and local partners to turn Wayve’s AI Driver into a safe, reliable robotaxi service, keeping complex, multi-team, cross-geography delivery predictable in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment. As the only TPM in Japan, you’ll also help support local vehicle hardware and software programs as needed. 🧩 What you’ll be working on: - Owning the lifecycle of robotaxi programs, from vehicle and fleet readiness to launch and in-market scaling, improving delivery through structured execution and coordination. - Tracking and managing dependencies, risks and trade-offs across autonomy, vehicle, operations and safety, and escalating early with clear options. - Keeping engineering, fleet operations, safety and local partner teams aligned, bridging Wayve’s global R&D with local delivery in Japan. - Providing hands-on program support for robotaxi launch and in-market operations, on the ground in Yokohama. - Supporting local vehicle hardware and software programs as needed, as the only TPM in Japan. - Running planning, stand-ups, reviews and retros that keep multi-team delivery predictable and reliable. - Communicating progress and risk to engineers, partners and senior leaders, tailoring updates to each audience. - Improving tooling, processes and ways of working, and sharing patterns across the TPM org. 🙌 You should apply if: Essential - Bias for action: 5+ years leading complex, multi-team programs (or a strong record at this level). You lead high-complexity work across teams and get things done. - Technically strong across software and hardware: Credible with engineers across autonomy, vehicle software, and vehicle hardware; you reason about how the AI Driver runs as a robotaxi service on real vehicles - and can support vehicle hardware / software programs too - even though you won't write production code. - Stays neutral and adapts under pressure: Effective in ambiguous, safety-critical environments; you flex your style and bring structure without slowing delivery. - Strong project management: Skilled at planning, prioritising, and managing risk and dependencies across teams. - Exceptional communicator & collaborator: Operating from a remote office, you keep distributed teams aligned across time zones - communicating with clarity, proactively and in writing - and influence without authority across all stakeholders. - Cross-cultural & on the ground: Comfortable working across geographies and cultures, and hands-on with vehicles and operations in Japan; Japanese language skills a plus. - Generalist range: As the only TPM in-country, comfortable flexing beyond robotaxi to support vehicle hardware and software programs; happy wearing multiple hats. - Data-driven: Comfortable defining and using metrics to steer delivery and decisions. - Growth mindset: Open to feedback and always looking to improve. - Systems thinking: You reason about how the parts of a complex system fit together, and how a change in one area ripples into others. - Product-minded: You focus on outcomes and the people your programs serve - prioritising by impact and defining what good looks like, not just tracking activity. Desirable - Robotaxi / AV operations, fleet, mobility, ride-hailing, or automotive experience; experience launching or scaling a service in market. - Exposure to ML, autonomy, or other compute-heavy / safety-critical domains; experience working across global and local teams. Japanese language skills and/or based in (or willing to relocate to) Yokohama / Tokyo. - An engineering or computer science degree, or experience working as an engineer. 🌱 Not ticking every box? That’s totally okay! If you’re passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every requirement. More about Wayve: 🚀 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end to end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale. Instead of relying on hand coded rules or pre mapped environments, our AI Driver learns to drive by understanding the world around it. The result is technology that navigates complex urban environments with intelligence, precision and natural flow, unlocking meaningful advances in both safety and efficiency. We believe autonomy represents a once in a generation transformation in how people and goods move, comparable to the shift from horses to cars, and from human driven vehicles to intelligent machines. Our ambition is to make autonomy universal. Wayve’s mapless and hardware agnostic AI platform integrates with global OEM partners, enabling continuous software evolution and unlocking advanced levels of automation from L2 plus through to L4 as our core AI model scales. In a race increasingly defined by intelligence and real world learning, Wayve is taking a distinct approach, building a generalisable driving intelligence that can power any vehicle, anywhere. By combining embodied AI with scalable deployment, we are creating technology that can be shaped to each OEM brand and driver experience, accelerating the transition to a safer, more intelligent future of mobility.
