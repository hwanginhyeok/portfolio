## JD 본문

Physical Intelligence is bringing general-purpose AI into the physical world. We are a group of engineers, scientists, roboticists, and company builders developing learning algorithms to create a model that will control any robot to do any task. The Team The Set Team plans, builds, and maintains these environments so research can run on real-world tasks. "Sets" are the physical rooms and scenes where our robots collect data: kitchens, living rooms, evaluation stations, and more. As a Set Team Specialist, you'll keep active sets organized, staged, and inventory-accurate so research runs without interruption. In This Role You Will - Audit Sets Daily: Check active sets every day for organization and cleanliness, working alongside the operator team. - Stage & Log: Stage props for new sets and log them accurately in inventory. - Flag Issues: Follow a defined checklist and escalate missing props, sets out of spec, and inventory mismatches to the Set Team Coordinator. - Support Prop Running: Deliver props and kits to operators when needed. What We Hope You'll Bring - 1+ year in retail merchandising, warehouse, stagehand, hospitality, or facilities work. - Strong attention to detail and reliability. - Comfort with basic inventory or tracking tools. - Ability to stand, lift up to 40 lbs, and move around for a full shift. Pursuant to the San Francisco Fair Chance Ordinance, we will consider for employment qualified applicants with arrest and conviction records.
