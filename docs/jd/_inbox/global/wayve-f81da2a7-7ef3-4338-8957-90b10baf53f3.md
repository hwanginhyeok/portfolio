## JD 본문

Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ ABOUT OUR ENGINEERING TEAMS The OS team builds and maintains the operating-system foundations for Wayve’s onboard software stack. Other vehicle-software components depend on the frameworks, observability capabilities and foundational layers owned by this team. The team works closely with Vehicle Software, model developers, hardware teams and OEM programes to enable Wayve’s AI Driver across new vehicles, compute platforms and other embodiments. As Engineering Manager, you’ll lead a single team and be accountable for its delivery, engagement, resourcing, roadmap and performance. People leadership will be your first priority, supported by the technical judgement needed to set direction and own the outcomes of the team’s decisions. 🧠 YOUR DAY-TO-DAY You’ll coach and develop engineers, maintain a healthy and high-performing team, and lead hiring and onboarding as the team grows. You’ll establish the team’s direction, identify the work with the greatest business impact and align the roadmap with available resources. In partnership with technical leads and stakeholders, you’ll guide technical decisions across system design, implementation, testing, deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement. You’ll also serve as the team’s escalation point, proactively identifying risks, dependencies and opportunities to unblock delivery. 🧩 WHAT YOU’LL BE WORKING ON - Leading and developing the team: Run effective one-to-ones, set clear expectations, provide regular high-quality feedback, manage performance and support career growth against Wayve’s career framework - Hiring and building the team: Own hiring and onboarding, ensure the right people are in the right roles, and shape the team’s culture and ways of working - Driving execution: Staff committed programmes, prioritise effectively and hold the team accountable for delivery, quality and operational reliability - Setting technical direction: Establish a clear, holistic technical direction and ownership of technology-roadmap milestones in partnership with technical leads and stakeholders - Owning Wayve’s OS foundations: Guide the operating-system roadmap and the team’s work across architecture, implementation, testing, deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement - Enabling new hardware platforms: Support new platforms through incremental development while minimising performance degradation - Strengthening quality and iteration: Implement and maintain strong continuous integration, hardware-in-the-loop testing and internal quality gates - Bringing up new boards: Collaborate with hardware partners on board bring-up and scale new platforms with quality - Improving build and deployment systems: Optimise build, packaging and deployment systems for broad capabilities and highly heterogeneous environments - Scaling safely: Establish quality metrics and automated testing and release gates that support deployments ranging from one to 10,000 units - Collaborating across Wayve: Partner with Vehicle Software, model development, hardware and OEM programe teams to deliver through influence and resolve cross-functional dependencies 🙌 YOU SHOULD APPLY IF - You have a strong track record of leading, growing, engaging and retaining a high-performing software or systems team - You have experience hiring, coaching, providing feedback, managing performance and supporting engineers’ career development - You have owned a team roadmap and delivered through others, including planning, prioritisation, resourcing and accountability for quality and operational reliability - You have hands-on experience architecting and delivering embedded software shipped in a physical product to real customers - You understand high-performance, low-latency embedded software, preferably developed in C or C++, and can earn the trust of senior engineers without needing to be the team’s top individual contributor - You have a solid understanding of Linux kernel fundamentals, device trees, board support packages, embedded build and packaging systems, software updates and over-the-air deployment - You understand QNX fundamentals, security and robust software-in-the-loop and hardware-in-the-loop testing - You have broad experience developing reliable, high-performance peripheral drivers across low- and high-speed devices, including technologies such as PCIe - You can set direction and deliver through influence across multiple stakeholder teams - You have experience managing direct reports across different time zones and leading projects involving external partners or vendors Experience with software and hardware architectures that implement high-reliability fault tolerance would be valuable. The following experience would also be useful but is not required: - NVIDIA DriveOS - Microkernel or real-time operating systems such as QNX - NVIDIA Jetson or Drive platforms - Qualcomm, AMD or Renesas compute platforms 🌱 Not ticking every box? That’s totally okay. If you’re passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every requirement. MORE ABOUT WAYVE 🚀 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end-to-end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real-world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale. Instead of relying on hand-coded rules or pre-mapped environments, our AI Driver learns to drive by understanding the world around it. The result is technology that navigates complex urban environments with intelligence, precision and natural flow, unlocking meaningful advances in both safety and efficiency. We believe autonomy represents a once-in-a-generation transformation in how people and goods move, comparable to the shift from horses to cars and from human-driven vehicles to intelligent machines. Our ambition is to make autonomy universal. Wayve’s mapless and hardware-agnostic AI platform integrates with global OEM partners, enabling continuous software evolution and unlocking advanced levels of automation from L2+ through to L4 as our core AI model scales. In a race increasingly defined by intelligence and real-world learning, Wayve is taking a distinct approach: building generalisable driving intelligence that can power any vehicle, anywhere. By combining embodied AI with scalable deployment, we are creating technology that can be shaped to each OEM brand and driver experience, accelerating the transition to a safer and more intelligent future of mobility. 💻 HOW WE WORK—LOCATION AND FLEXIBLE WORKING This is a full-time, hybrid role based in Sunnyvale, California.
