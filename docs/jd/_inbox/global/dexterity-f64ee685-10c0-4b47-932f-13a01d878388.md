We are seeking a motivated, experienced individual to fill the role of Manufacturing Engineer, Document Control. In this role, you will manage all aspects of Engineering Change Order (ECO) and Bill of Material (BOM) management, ensuring that these critical documents are accurate, up-to-date, and complete. This includes creating, updating, and maintaining BOMs, coordinating configuration management processes, and managing BOM changes through rigorous change control. Your close collaboration with Engineering, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain teams will enable you to verify component availability and specifications, along with recommending cost-saving opportunities and process improvements. This role requires a combination of technical expertise, project management skills, and cross-functional collaboration to ensure the accuracy and efficiency of our product information management systems and successfully bring products to volume manufacturing and meet market demands.
