Role Overview We are looking for a Perception Software Engineer to design, integrate, and deploy ML- based perception software within our ADAS stack. This engineer will collaborate with ML engineers, embedded software developers, and systems teams to deliver production- ready solutions across Lucid’s vehicle platforms. Responsibilities • Research, develop, and integrate bird’s-eye-view (BEV) transformer models for perception tasks. • Research and develop state-of-the-art machine-learning-based prediction solutions for production perception systems. • Develop and maintain comprehensive unit and integration tests. • Deploy and optimize ML models on embedded platforms. • Write production-grade C++ code aligned with MISRA/AUTOSAR standards. • Develop and maintain diagnostic pipelines for perception modules. • Debug and resolve static analysis violations and performance bottlenecks. • Collaborate with multi-disciplinary teams across ML, perception, systems, and safety. Qualifications • Strong foundation in software integration, operating systems used in embedded environments, including QNX and Ubuntu, and embedded software development. • Strong theoretical foundation and practical expertise in deep-learning algorithms for dynamic and static object detection, tracking, and segmentation. • Experience contributing to large, complex production codebases in a collaborative development environment. • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or a related field. • Minimum of three years of relevant work experience. Candidates considered for a senior-level position should have additional relevant industry experience or a Ph.D. in a related field.
