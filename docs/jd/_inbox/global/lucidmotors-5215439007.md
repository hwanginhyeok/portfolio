Overview Job Title: Senior Software Engineer, Access Controls &nbsp; About&nbsp;the Team &nbsp; The Body Controls team&nbsp;is responsible for&nbsp;many of the vehicle features customers use every day, including access and security, closures, windows, mirrors, lighting, seating, power management, and diagnostics. The team develops and supports software across multiple ECUs and vehicle platforms, ensuring high-quality customer experiences from development through production and service. &nbsp; This is a highly cross-functional team that works on embedded software development, diagnostics, vehicle integration, supplier management, new hardware bring-up, and production support. The team owns end-to-end delivery of body electronics features across multiple vehicle programs, offering engineers broad technical exposure and significant ownership of customer-facing vehicle functionality. &nbsp; Responsibilities: Develop control systems for vehicle access control features; this includes vehicle lock/unlock, access device authentication, device localization through advanced&nbsp;controls&nbsp;algorithms such as&nbsp;kalman&nbsp;filters and bi/trilateralization. &nbsp; Develop requirements and related test cases for access control and other assigned features modeled in&nbsp;Matlab/Simulink. &nbsp; Collaborate with simulation engineers to improve or develop a simulation model/platform to analyze, optimize, and troubleshoot access control and localization algorithms. &nbsp; Assist&nbsp;in the integration of features into the controller code base. Test the developed software on the vehicle and&nbsp;assist&nbsp;in calibration of the features. &nbsp; Support Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA) for various features to&nbsp;comply with&nbsp;ISO 26262 standards. Develop diagnostic, communication, and other interfaces across controllers/ECUs. Work in cross-functional teams to solve&nbsp;controls&nbsp;problems. &nbsp; Participate in the design of a world-class engineering effort to bring electric vehicles to a new level of refinement. &nbsp; What You Bring &nbsp; Minimum Qualifications: &nbsp; Bachelor's degree (or higher) in computer engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, automotive systems, or related technical fields is required. &nbsp; 4+ years of experience with automotive control systems&nbsp;and knowledge of advanced&nbsp;controls&nbsp;algorithms; experience with vehicle access/ingress/egress control features and RF/BLE/UWB technologies is a plus. &nbsp; Expert knowledge of model-based design using&nbsp;Matlab/Simulink/Stateflow. &nbsp; Expert understanding of Automatic Control Systems, with a firm grasp of classical control theory tools like compensator design, Kalman Filtering, and PID tuning. &nbsp; Proficiency&nbsp;in C/C++ and&nbsp;integration into embedded ECUs &nbsp; Experience with&nbsp;industry standard development&nbsp;tools&nbsp;such as&nbsp;JAMA/JIRA/Git/Confluence. &nbsp; Experience in&nbsp;working with cross-functional teams. Creativity in design, hands-on ability, and&nbsp;strong communication&nbsp;skills. &nbsp; &nbsp; Preferred Qualifications: Experience with RF/BLE/UWB. &nbsp; Experience with&nbsp;kalman&nbsp;filters, machine&nbsp;learning&nbsp;and clustering techniques. &nbsp; Common understanding&nbsp;of ISO 26262 and&nbsp;Autosar. &nbsp; Experience in designing controllers for electric vehicle systems. &nbsp; Experience with CAN analysis tools like CANalyzer and&nbsp;CANape.
