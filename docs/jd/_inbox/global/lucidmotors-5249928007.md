Position Overview &nbsp; This role connects Product Development, Manufacturing, Quality, Service, and Supplier teams to support the successful launch, deployment, and continuous improvement of ADAS hardware, software, and vehicle-level functionality throughout the production lifecycle. The ideal candidate brings strong technical knowledge of vehicle systems, diagnostics, and manufacturing processes, combined with a hands-on approach to solving problems in a fast-paced production environment. &nbsp; Key Responsibilities &nbsp; Lead the investigation and resolution of ADAS-related manufacturing, assembly, and vehicle quality issues. &nbsp; Conduct root cause analysis across sensors, ECUs, vehicle networks, software, and calibration processes. &nbsp; Perform vehicle-level integration testing, software verification, and manufacturing trials to support issue resolution and product validation. &nbsp; Support new product launches, engineering changes, and manufacturing readiness for ADAS features and components. &nbsp; Manage part concessions, deviations, and change control activities for ADAS hardware and software. &nbsp; Support End-of-Line diagnostics, calibration, software flashing, and production validation. &nbsp; Partner with Product Development, Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, Service, and Supplier teams to resolve issues and drive continuous improvement. &nbsp; Improve ADAS manufacturing processes, troubleshooting procedures, and validation methods. &nbsp; Provide technical guidance and training to Manufacturing and Quality teams. &nbsp; Support production escalations and ensure critical issues are closed on time. &nbsp; Skills &nbsp; Strong analytical problem-solving and root cause analysis skills. &nbsp; Clear communication, documentation, and cross-functional collaboration skills. &nbsp; Hands-on experience with vehicle diagnostics and ECU flashing. &nbsp; Knowledge of automotive electrical and network architecture. &nbsp; Working knowledge of CAN, Automotive Ethernet, and diagnostic protocols. &nbsp; Experience with CANoe , CANalyzer, CANape , or similar engineering tools. &nbsp; Ability to work independently with minimal supervision in a fast-paced production environment. &nbsp; Strong ownership, resilience, and a proactive problem-solving mindset. &nbsp; Strong organizational and project management skills. &nbsp; Ability to support production activities outside regular business hours when needed. &nbsp; Minimum Qualifications &nbsp; Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or a related technical field. &nbsp; 5+ years of experience in automotive manufacturing, production engineering, systems integration, diagnostics, or quality. &nbsp; Experience troubleshooting vehicle electrical, software, or communication issues. &nbsp; Familiarity with ECU diagnostics, software flashing, and vehicle verification. &nbsp; Working knowledge of vehicle communication protocols, including CAN, Automotive Ethernet, and UDS. &nbsp; Experience with manufacturing quality methods, including PFMEA, Control Plans, and structured problem solving. &nbsp; Willingness and ability to travel domestically and internationally as required . &nbsp; ADAS and Sensor Systems &nbsp; Experience with ADAS, Autonomous Driving systems, and sensor technologies such as cameras, radar, lidar, or related perception hardware. &nbsp; Tools and Process Experience &nbsp;
