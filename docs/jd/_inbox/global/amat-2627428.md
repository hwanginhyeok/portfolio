Position Overview The EHS Manager will lead Environment, Health, and Safety support for Applied Materials Korea’s Samsung account. This role ensures safe and compliant field operations, manages customer-facing EHS issues, drives execution of Applied Materials’ EHS programs, and leads a team of Safety Engineers. The position reports to the Korea Regional EHS Manager. Key Responsibilities Lead EHS support for the Samsung account, including field service, customer fab operations, project execution, and issue resolution. Manage, coach, and develop Safety Engineers by setting clear priorities, providing guidance, and supporting career growth. Partner with field teams, account management, business leaders, and Samsung stakeholders to enable safe, compliant, and efficient operations. Implement Applied Materials’ EHS programs and controls, including risk assessment, JHA, PTP, contractor safety, MOC, chemical safety, LOTO, electrical safety, fall prevention, and incident prevention. Review high-risk work, identify hazards, define controls, and support effective execution of safe work practices in customer fab environments. Provide EHS assessment and support for projects, including real estate initiatives, facility changes, and other business-critical projects requiring EHS review and risk mitigation. Lead or support incident investigations, operational learning, root cause analysis, corrective actions, and closure tracking. Monitor Korean EHS regulatory and customer requirement changes and translate them into practical guidance for field operations. Provide EHS performance updates, risk summaries, escalation items, and improvement plans to EHS and business leadership. Functional Knowledge Strong technical knowledge of occupational safety, field safety, semiconductor fab hazards, contractor safety, and customer site requirements. Solid understanding of Korean EHS regulations and customer-facing field execution requirements. Practical experience with EHS management systems, risk assessment, incident investigation, corrective action management, and field controls. Business Expertise Understands how EHS supports field execution, customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and business continuity. Balances compliance, customer expectations, operational urgency, and practical field constraints. Experience in semiconductor, display, manufacturing, construction, equipment installation, or high-risk technical service environments is strongly preferred. Leadership Leads a team of Safety Engineers by setting clear priorities, removing barriers, developing capability, and ensuring quality execution. Acts as a trusted EHS partner to field leadership and customer-facing teams, influencing safe decision-making without slowing down business-critical operations. Demonstrates ownership, accountability, sound judgment, and the ability to escalate risks and issues appropriately. Interpersonal Skills Communicates clearly and professionally with internal field teams/SPG-GFG, business leaders, customer stakeholders, and regional or global EHS teams. Influences others through credibility, practical judgment, collaboration, and a customer-focused mindset. Demonstrates integrity, accountability, cultural sensitivity, and the ability to build trust across functions and organizational levels.
