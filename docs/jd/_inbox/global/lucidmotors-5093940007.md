Your Role:&nbsp; Design, develop, and maintain scalable automation frameworks and tooling to orchestrate ADAS/AD SIL (Software-in-Loop) and HIL (Hardware-in-Loop) simulations, including test execution, result collection, and reporting.&nbsp; Design and maintain tooling for large-scale regression execution, including scheduling, failure triage, log management, and metrics extraction.&nbsp; Support scenario-based validation by enabling tooling for scenario configuration, parameterization, execution, and result evaluation.&nbsp; Be responsible for creating tools for creating data visualizations either from scratch or leveraging open-source solutions such as Foxglove&nbsp; Update and maintain the automation tools based on feedback from users of the HIL and SIL system &nbsp; Develop an understanding of HIL and SIL simulation setup for L2++ and L3 ADAS/AD driving, safety, and parking functions&nbsp; Develop, maintain, and scale CI/CD pipelines for ADAS simulation workflows (e.g., per-commit smoke tests, nightly regressions), including integration with version control, build systems, and simulation farms.&nbsp; Monitor pipeline health, reduce execution time, and improve overall simulation reliability and debuggability.&nbsp; Support HIL and SIL development as necessary&nbsp; Work with suppliers as necessary and represent Lucid ADAS systems validation team.&nbsp; Expectations:&nbsp; Very good understanding of software development for automation tools, post-processing and application development&nbsp; Highly proficient in Python; working knowledge of MATLAB for analysis, modeling, and automation &nbsp; Working experience with object-oriented software design and API development&nbsp; Experience and comfort working with data from different sources and in different formats&nbsp; Strong experience working in Linux-based development environments, including shell scripting and automation &nbsp; Experience with containerization and environment management (Docker or similar)&nbsp; Basic understanding of ADAS/AD features or other physical systems&nbsp; Capability and creativity to successfully complete projects with unconventional ideas in highly constrained, innovative startup environments&nbsp; Excellent communication skills (verbal and written) &amp; ownership mindset&nbsp; Advantageous / Preferred Qualifications:&nbsp; Previous experience working in HIL and SIL setups for validating ADAS features at OEM or Tier 1&nbsp; Experience managing large volumes of simulation data, logs, and performance metrics&nbsp; Experience with C++ for simulation interfaces or performance-critical tooling&nbsp; Experience with scenario standards and formats such as OpenSCENARIO and OpenDRIVE&nbsp; Experience integrating simulation tools with ROS2 or similar middleware&nbsp; Understanding of SOTIF, ISO 26262, NCAP&nbsp; Broad understanding of various ADAS/AD systems and the interactions between mechanical, electrical and software components within the system&nbsp; Proficiency with agile development tools and environments (Jira, Jama, …)&nbsp; Requirements:&nbsp; BS minimum in System Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or similar and 3+ years of engineering experience in the automotive industry OR MS minimum in System Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or similar and 1+ years of engineering experience in the automotive industry&nbsp;
