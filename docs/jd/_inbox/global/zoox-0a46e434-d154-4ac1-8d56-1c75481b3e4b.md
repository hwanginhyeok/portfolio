## JD 본문

As a Team Lead for Readiness and Response within Zoox’s Fleet Operations organization, you are at the forefront of the autonomous mobility revolution. You will be a key leader in our expansion into new markets, responsible for the end-to-end operational integrity of the Zoox Robotaxi and Mapping Vehicle Fleet. This role unifies the technical preparation of our vehicles (Readiness) with high-level oversight of live field incidents (Response), ensuring every Zoox vehicle is "service ready" and every mission is supported with operational excellence. Your leadership will bridge the gap between structured depot workflows and the dynamic environment of real-time fleet response. Rather than performing hands-on technical tasks, you will drive strategy and performance by managing a hybrid workforce of Full-Time Shift Leads and contingent associates. Serving as the primary on-site escalation path for critical field incidents and holding third-party recovery partners accountable, you will directly impact Zoox’s fleet availability, safety standards, and our ability to deliver a seamless experience to our riders.
