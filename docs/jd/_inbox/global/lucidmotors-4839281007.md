Position Overview The Manager, Powertrain Production Operations owns end-to-end 24/7 production execution across AMP-2 Powertrain — covering both Drive Unit and HV Battery assembly shops. Joining a greenfield facility, this leader establishes the entire production organization from scratch: recruiting and developing all shifts employees, embedding shift routines, and driving performance from first commissioning through volume ramp-up to and beyond SOP. The role is accountable for Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost, and People. The role operates through disciplined change control, objective launch evidence, cross-functional problem solving, and structured transfer of technical ownership to the Saudi workforce. Key Responsibilities Strategic Production Leadership Own 24/7 production execution for DU and HV Battery assembly shops across all shifts Deliver committed production volume, OEE, FPY, and cost-per-unit targets aligned to AMP-2 business plan Own operational budget including labor, overtime, consumables, and conversion cost performance Build the production organization from ground up: shift managers, supervisors, team leads, operators Launch Governance &amp; Ramp-Up Own Safe Launch, run-at-rate, and capacity verification governance through SOP and volume ramp Chair Launch Readiness Reviews, Layered Process Audits, and Process Confirmation routines Lead QRQC / War Room escalation during launch and ramp-up phases Ensure operational readiness for future powertrain variants and product introductions Operational Excellence &amp; KPI Ownership Define, cascade, and govern production KPIs — Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost, People and Environment (SQDCPE) Lead tiered accountability (Tier 1–3) daily management routines across the Powertrain shop Drive structured problem-solving and QRQC closure for chronic and acute production issues Own S&amp;OP execution interface with Supply Chain, Quality, and Engineering functions HV Safety &amp;
