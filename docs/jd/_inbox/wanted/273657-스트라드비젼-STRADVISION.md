## 주요업무

[Mission of the Role] 
• As a Technical Project Manager, you will spearhead, lead, and navigate the development and execution of AD and ADAS projects, ensuring they are delivered within the designated timelines, budget constraints, and quality standards to meet customer satisfaction. Your role is pivotal in bridging the gap between complex technical requirements and project execution, managing the internal software development lifecycle to drive successful product innovation and development.
• In this capacity, you will effectively communicate with both customers and cross-functional project teams, orchestrating schedules, and proactively managing issues and risks. You will lead project members with a technical perspective, fostering a collaborative environment that encourages innovation and problem-solving. 

[Key Responsibilities]
• Direct the full project lifecycle, coordinating with data engineers, software engineers, platform engineers, validation engineers, and key stakeholders to ensure timely delivery within scope and budget.
• Serve as the primary technical contact for customers, balancing requirements and expectations with feature development, milestone planning, and risk management.
• Oversee software development planning and requirements analysis. Ensure continuous tracking of progress and adapt plans as necessary.
• Proactively identify, prioritize, and manage project issues and risks, aligning engineering resources and resolving discrepancies to maintain project trajectory.
• Regularly evaluate project metrics (e.g., customer satisfaction, timing, compliance with specifications, budget, and quality) and communicate status updates to stakeholders.
• Guarantee that all project activities adhere to relevant standards and processes, enhancing efficiency and quality of output.
• Negotiate and oversee change requests, aligning customer needs with project capabilities and resources.
• Conduct and facilitate essential meetings, including customer discussions in English, project team meetings to ensure alignment and progress.
• Exhibit exceptional multitasking skills to manage overlapping tasks, competing priorities, and dynamic project requirements effectively.
