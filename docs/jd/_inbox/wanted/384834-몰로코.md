## 소개

Moloco builds some of the most powerful AI advertising solutions in the world. Our name—short for "machine learning company"—reflects our core mission: democratizing access to the advanced AI that has historically been reserved for tech giants. Led by machine learning pioneers who built some of the most successful ad systems at Google, including YouTube's monetization engine and key search advertising technologies, we're transforming how businesses grow and compete in the digital economy.

Built with AI from day one, Moloco’s planet-scale machine learning platform powers a suite of solutions for advertising growth and monetization. Moloco Ads is an AI-powered platform that delivers real business outcomes for mobile app marketers through performance-based user acquisition. Moloco Commerce Media enables retailers and marketplaces to build revenue-generating ad businesses that balance user experience and advertiser performance.

Moloco is headquartered in Silicon Valley, with offices in Seattle, New York, San Francisco, Seoul, Beijing, Singapore, Gurgaon, Tokyo, Shanghai, London, Tel Aviv, and Berlin.

Moloco is a truly rewarding place to work and in an exciting period of growth, which you could be a part of. Join us today and apply now!

## 주요업무

[ The Impact You'll Be Contributing to Moloco ]
Moloco helps businesses grow through machine learning-powered advertising and measurement. As a TPM supporting our CTV business, you will help shape how CTV products and platforms scale across app, web, and smart TV environments.

You will make an impact by:
• Connecting business goals to clear priorities, milestones, and execution plans.
• Creating visibility into performance, investment, operational health, and progress against goals.
• Aligning Product, Engineering, Data, Finance, and Go-to-Market teams around shared outcomes.
• Coordinating identity, measurement, reporting, and platform initiatives that improve the CTV experience for customers and partners.
• Helping global teams move with greater clarity and predictability across Seoul and other Moloco offices.

[ The Opportunity ]
As the TPM for CTV, you will lead complex, cross-functional programs from problem definition through launch and post-launch learning. You will partner with Product, Engineering, Infrastructure, Data Science and Machine Learning, Finance, Strategy, Go-to-Market, and customer-facing teams across Moloco.

You will:
• Translate CTV strategy and customer needs into a focused roadmap, well-defined workstreams, milestones, and dependencies.
• Establish clear planning and communication rhythms that keep teams aligned and leaders informed.
• Drive execution across technical and business initiatives, bringing structure to ambiguity and momentum to high-priority work.
• Identify risks, surface trade-offs, and guide decisions to resolution through influence and strong stakeholder partnership.
• Connect launch plans with measurable outcomes, using data and feedback to improve products and operating practices.
• Build effective collaboration across Seoul and Moloco’s global teams.
