• Performing valuation analyses
• Working on mergers and acquisitions
• Building detailed financial models and executing transactions from pitch to close
• Analyzing market data, building detailed financial models and preparing client presentations
• Coming up with innovative and creative ways to solve complex, real-world business challenges
• Learning how we help clients and communities to grow and polishing your technical skills
• Building your professional network with mentors, senior executives and others

## 자격요건

• Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting or other related disciplines

• Excellent analytical, quantitative and communication skills
• Good understanding of the M&A and capital markets transaction cycle and processes involved
• Excellent leadership and collaborative skills
• Knowing your way around Excel, PowerPoint and Word
• Fluency in Korean language skills since this role will be supporting & interacting with Korean clients
