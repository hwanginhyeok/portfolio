## 소개

Play a visible role in keeping risk governance and regulatory deliverables running smoothly across a busy operating environment. This role offers exposure to risk governance frameworks, recurring reporting cycles and senior stakeholder interactions. You will be trusted with high-quality documentation, coordination and follow-through that directly supports the control environment. It is a strong opportunity to build practical risk and governance capability while partnering closely with Risk, Compliance and Legal.

## 주요업무

• Coordinate risk governance, regulatory reporting and monitoring deliverables, including timelines, inputs and follow-ups
• Maintain governance documentation, trackers and supporting records across Risk, Compliance and Legal
• Prepare and consolidate meeting packs and recurring reporting materials, performing completeness and consistency checks
• Track actions, issues and outstanding items, driving timely closure through proactive stakeholder engagement
• Escalate items requiring review or decision to the appropriate functional stakeholders
• Support updates to governance frameworks, procedures and standard templates to improve consistency and efficiency
• Assist with monitoring activities and evidence collection to support control and oversight requirements
• Build an understanding of the legal entity's activities, risk profile and applicable regulatory requirements
• Support governance forums by coordinating agendas, logistics, minutes and action logs (as required)
• Partner effectively across functions, ensuring clear communication and aligned priorities
• Provide ad-hoc support to Risk, Compliance and Legal as reasonably required

## 자격요건

• Demonstrated experience coordinating recurring deliverables with multiple deadlines and stakeholders
• Demonstrated experience producing high-quality documentation and maintaining trackers/records with strong attention to detail
• Working knowledge of governance processes, controls, or regulatory reporting/monitoring in a corporate environment
• Strong organization, reliability, and follow-through, with the ability to prioritize effectively
• Clear written and verbal communication skills, with confidence engaging cross-functional stakeholders
• Sound judgment in identifying when to escalate issues or seek guidance
• Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint and Word for reporting and materials preparation
• Ability to work collaboratively and professionally in a fast-paced environment
• Intellectual curiosity and willingness to learn risk and governance concepts quickly

## 우대사항

• Experience supporting Risk, Compliance or Legal teams in a regulated financial services environment
• Experience supporting governance committees, including preparing packs and maintaining action logs
• Familiarity with regulatory interactions and evidence management for reporting cycles
• Experience improving processes, templates, or reporting routines to reduce execution risk
• Experience working in a multi-entity or matrixed regional environment.
