## 소개

As a Corporate Banker within the Global Corporate Banking APAC Subsidiary Banking team, you will support the coverage and growth of a defined portfolio of multinational subsidiary clients across the globe. This position requires a role where you will hold ownership of client accounts as a Relationship Manager and provide direct coverage support to Senior Bankers on more complex relationships. You will partner closely with global/regional/in-country bankers, product partners, and middle/back office teams to originate, execute, and ramp client mandates, delivering well-rounded corporate banking coverage across cash management, FX, trade finance, and credit/lending.

## 주요업무

• Responsible for day-to-day relationship management and coverage for a defined portfolio of subsidiary banking clients, including proactive client engagement and responsiveness to client needs.
• Support Senior Subsidiary Bankers in managing broader/complex client relationships, including meeting preparation, follow-ups, and execution of agreed coverage strategies.
• Drive disciplined pipeline management for your portfolio: maintain accurate tracking, prioritize opportunities, and progress deals from identification through execution and ramp.
• Originate and help execute new business opportunities by identifying client needs across the full corporate banking spectrum (cash management, payments, liquidity, FX, trade finance, and credit/lending) and coordinating internal resources to deliver solutions.
• Act as a key internal coordinator across headquarter/global/regional/in-country bankers, product partners, and middle/back office functions to ensure timely decision-making, approvals, and high-quality client delivery.
• Lead and/or support onboarding and ramp-up of new business mandates, ensuring clear accountability across stakeholders and strong control of timelines, dependencies, and issue resolution.
• Prepare relationship reviews and client analyses, including wallet/penetration discussions, pipeline updates, and monitoring of portfolio profitability and ramp progression.
• Maintain a strong understanding of Know Your Customer (KYC) responsibilities and requirements; support KYC record renewals, coordinate required information, and provide accurate summaries to in-country bankers and relevant stakeholders.
• Serve as a dependable point of contact for portfolio matters, ensuring consistent communication internally and externally and reinforcing the firm's standards of client coverage.
• Through disciplined coverage and pipeline hygiene, identify incremental revenue streams and ensure client requirements are met to the highest standard.

## 자격요건

• Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in corporate banking services, relationship management, transaction banking, and/or portfolio management.
• Well-rounded product knowledge and client coverage capability across corporate banking solutions, including cash management, transactional FX, trade finance, and credit/lending.
• Excellent communication skills with strong teamwork ability; able to work effectively across global/regional/in-country stakeholders and product partners.
• Strong organizational, planning, problem-solving, time management, and analytical skills.
• Meticulous attention to detail with the ability to multi-task and manage competing priorities to meet deadlines and exceed expectations.
• Proactive self-starter with a strong work ethic, ownership mindset, and desire to learn; able to operate with increasing independence while leveraging senior coverage guidance.
• Demonstrated professional presence and sound judgment, with the ability to adapt to evolving client and business needs.
• Proven ability to coordinate across functions to resolve issues efficiently, manage escalations appropriately, and deliver a high-quality client experience.
