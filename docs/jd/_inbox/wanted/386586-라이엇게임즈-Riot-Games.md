## 소개

The Riot Esports team is focused on delivering amazing fan experiences through global events and regional competitions. Our professional and semi-professional competitive tiers in League of Legends, VALORANT and other Esports involve thousands of players and hundreds of organizations competing every week. As an Esports Product Manager - Riftbound, you will work on the The TFT & Emerging Esports team. This team is focused on developing new esports and competitions for Riot.

## 주요업무

• Translate Korea market insights, program results, and player and partner feedback into recommendations for the Korea Riftbound OP strategy and roadmap.
• Partner with Global Riftbound Organized Play team to balance global consistency with Korea-specific needs and share Korea learnings across APAC.
• Deliver and continuously improve Korea's competitive pathway, from store play and third-party competitions to premier regional events and global qualification.
• Partner with Publishing on prizing strategies that balance competitive prestige, collectibility, and long-term ecosystem health.
• Lead the delivery of Organized Play programs with local operators and vendors, managing scope, budget, timelines, risks, quality, and cross-functional communication as needed.
• Serve as the primary escalation point for rules, formats, eligibility, and integrity questions, escalating gameplay issues and compliance or technical risks to the appropriate Riot teams.
• Build strategic relationships with key Local Game Store (LGS) networks, tournament organizers, PCBs, judge communities, and other Organized Play partners.
• Support Riftbound play experiences at selected events, including Pre-Rift, Convergence Fest, and industry conventions.

## 자격요건

• 5+ years of experience in product management, program management, organized play, esports / sports tournament operation, or a related field.
• Experience delivering competitive programs or events, including working with vendors, external partners, budgets, and complex operations.
• Strong product and analytical skills, with the ability to develop strategy and translate player and business insights into execution.
• Excellent cross-functional and external stakeholder management skills.
• Working proficiency in Korean and English.

## 우대사항

• Experience working in the Trading Card Game (TCG) industry is strongly preferred
• Relevant experience designing and operating esports or sports competition programs is also valued
