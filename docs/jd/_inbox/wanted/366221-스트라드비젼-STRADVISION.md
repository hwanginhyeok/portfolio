## 주요업무

[Mission of the Role] 
STRADVISION’s Technical Project Management Group is responsible for the successful development and delivery of AD (Autonomous Driving) and ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) projects, serving as the bridge between engineering teams and customers. Within the organization, the Technical Project Manager acts not as a pure schedule manager, but as a hands-on technical leader who translates customer requirements into executable development plans, identifies and manages technical risks, and drives project execution from concept through vehicle-level validation and customer delivery. Specifically, the role contributes through the following objectives and responsibilities:

1. Program Planning and Customer Delivery
Define and manage the overall program scope, schedule, milestones, and deliverables to ensure successful customer delivery while meeting quality, performance, and timeline commitments.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Review customer technical specifications and Statements of Work (SOW), define project scope and deliverables, and maintain the master program plan by integrating schedules from multiple engineering teams. You will break milestones into sub-task-level schedules across the network-optimization, annotation/data, and calibration teams, and use that bottoms-up view to judge whether the proposed release cadence is realistic. Lead customer engagements and alignment activities to ensure successful milestone execution and software delivery.

2. System Requirements Management and Cross-Functional Alignment
Ensure customer requirements are translated into executable engineering activities and maintained throughout the development lifecycle.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Coordinate and oversee system-level requirements and traceability from customer needs to test cases. Partner with engineering leads across AI, perception, planning, and control teams to align development activities, validation strategies, and delivery objectives.

3. Technical Risk and Issue Management
Identify, assess, and proactively manage technical risks, dependencies, and blockers that may impact program execution and customer commitments.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Identify gaps between customer expectations and engineering feasibility, manage program-level risks, and monitor schedule, KPI targets, compute budget constraints, and technical dependencies. Coordinate root-cause analysis activities for technical blockers and vehicle-level failures by facilitating collaboration across engineering teams.

4. Software Integration and System Validation
Drive software integration, vehicle-level validation, and release readiness to ensure performance and quality requirements are achieved before customer delivery.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Coordinate software integration activities across engineering teams and monitor readiness for target ECUs/SoCs and vehicle platforms. Orchestrate each software release end to end, owning the delivery package while coordinating the supporting data strategy across logging, storage, labeling, and recalibration. Lead system-level validation planning, KPI tracking, and failure-case reviews across MIL, SIL, HIL, VIL, and real-road evaluation activities.

5. Stakeholder Management and Technical Communication
Facilitate effective communication between engineering teams, customers, and stakeholders by translating complex technical information into actionable decisions.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Serve as the single point of contact (SPOC) for internal and external stakeholders. Lead internal weekly meetings, external joint sync meetings, and technical workshops while driving alignment, decision-making, and issue resolution throughout the program lifecycle.

6. Process Compliance and Quality Assurance
Support automotive software development processes and ensure deliverables comply with industry standards and customer expectations.

＜Technical Project Manager Role＞
Collaborate with safety and cybersecurity teams to support ASPICE-oriented development processes and ensure deliverables meet ISO 26262, SOTIF, and ISO/SAE 21434 requirements. Facilitate trade-off discussions involving performance, schedule, KPI targets, and compute versus safety constraints.
