[Role Summary]

The Enterprise PMO Lead is a hands-on individual contributor responsible for establishing and operating the organization's enterprise-level project management foundation for strategic initiatives and major cross-functional projects.

The role creates a single view of priority initiatives, introduces practical governance and reporting, improves alignment to strategy and business outcomes, and enables effective cross-functional coordination.

Working through partnership and influence, the Enterprise PMO Lead supports executive sponsors, functional leaders, and project owners. Project delivery accountability remains with the designated sponsors and owners; this role enables visibility, discipline, escalation, and decision support.

[Key Responsibilities]
Enterprise Portfolio Visibility and Reporting
· Establish and maintain a consolidated enterprise view of strategically important initiatives, major projects, and cross-functional workstreams.
· Define practical criteria for which initiatives should be tracked at the enterprise level based on strategic importance, scale, risk, investment, complexity, or cross-functional impact.
· Track project ownership, milestones, status, risks, issues, dependencies, decisions, resource constraints, and target outcomes.
· Improve the quality, consistency, and reliability of project information so leadership has a clear single source of truth.

Governance and Executive Decision Support
· Establish and operate a lightweight governance cadence for priority initiatives, including leadership reviews, steering committee preparation, status reporting, decision logs, and follow-up tracking.
· Develop standard status definitions, dashboard formats, meeting materials, escalation paths, and decision-making routines.
· Prepare concise executive-level reporting on portfolio health, progress, risks, dependencies, and required decisions.
· Ensure governance supports accountability and timely decision-making without creating unnecessary administrative burden.

Strategic Alignment and Portfolio Coordination
· Partner with strategy, corporate planning, finance, and business leaders to ensure key initiatives remain aligned with corporate priorities and expected business outcomes.
· Support portfolio-level discussions on prioritization, trade-offs, timing, sequencing, dependencies, investment, and organizational capacity.
· Identify overlapping initiatives, competing priorities, resource constraints, and projects requiring leadership clarification.
· Support basic outcome, benefit, and KPI tracking for priority initiatives in partnership with initiative owners.

Cross-functional Coordination and Follow-up
· Facilitate alignment among business units, corporate functions, project sponsors, and project owners.
· Clarify roles, responsibilities, decision owners, handoffs, and required actions for cross-functional initiatives.
· Track dependencies and follow up on commitments that require coordination across multiple departments.
· Act as a neutral coordination point to help teams surface blockers early and resolve execution gaps.

Risk, Issue, Dependency, and Escalation Management
· Identify and track material risks, issues, blockers, dependencies, and decision points across enterprise-level projects.
· Work with project owners to document mitigation plans, recovery actions, owners, and timelines.
· Escalate material risks or stalled decisions with clear options and recommended next steps.
· Monitor follow-through on agreed actions and ensure key issues remain visible until resolved.

Project Management Framework Development and Adoption
· Develop practical standards, tools, templates, and guidance for project charters, workplans, milestones, status reports, risk logs, issue logs, decision logs, and action trackers.
· Help project owners apply appropriate project management practices based on project complexity, maturity, and business impact.
· Promote consistent project management discipline through coaching, facilitation, and hands-on support.
· Continuously improve the framework based on stakeholder feedback, leadership needs, adoption levels, and project outcomes.

[Key Deliverables]
· Enterprise initiative inventory and portfolio dashboard for strategic initiatives and major projects.
· Enterprise-level tracking criteria, initiative intake guidance, and leadership review criteria.
· Governance cadence with status definitions, escalation paths, decision logs, and follow-up process.
· Standard reporting templates and practical project management toolkit for project owners.
· Risk, issue, dependency, decision, and action tracking process.
· Executive reporting package with clear insights, risks, trade-offs, and required decisions.
· Basic outcome, benefit, and KPI tracking approach for priority initiatives.
