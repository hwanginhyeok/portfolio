## 소개

AWS Solutions Architects are trusted advisors to our customers, helping them solve some of their largest business challenges by designing and advising on some of the most scalable, flexible and resilient solutions in the world. Being a thought leader who creates and presents content you will test traditional thinking by pushing the boundaries for your customers to dream and deliver beyond what they think is possible. You will have the opportunity to partner with customers early in their cloud journey and help them scale further bringing visible and measurable impact in their growth ambitions in non-linear ways, using AWS as building blocks.

## 주요업무

• Work directly with customers to accelerate their business outcomes and recommend best-practice cloud architectures in line with their long-term business needs, going beyond what they say and into what they really need, and develop new solutions that best match those needs.
• Drive technical solutions discussions with your customers, diving deep into the details to solve complex technical challenges and craft scalable, flexible, and resilient cloud architectures.
• Act as a thought leader in wider community, playing a key role in educating, sharing best practices, presenting at events, writing white papers, blogs, and running workshops both in Korean and English.
• Deeply understand AWS platform and architectural best practices, and develop unique technical capability to become a go-to person for both customer and team.
• Serve as a subject matter expert to guide technology strategy by convincing decision makers across multiple domains from technical teams to executives.
• As a Builder, shape the direction of products and services through gathering feedback from customers whilst collaborating with engineering and service teams.

## 자격요건

• 8+ years of specific technology domain areas (e.g. software development, cloud computing, systems engineering, infrastructure, security, networking, data & analytics) experience.
• Strong verbal and written communication skills (Presentation for internal/external events, White boarding).
• Demonstrated ability to adapt to new technologies and learn quickly.
• Passion and experience for evangelizing and presenting technical concepts across technical and non-technical audiences and at C-level, including training, workshops, publications.

## 우대사항

• Experience migrating or transforming legacy customer solutions to the cloud.
